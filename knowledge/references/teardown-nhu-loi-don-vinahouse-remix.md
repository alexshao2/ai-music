# Teardown — "Như Lời Đồn (Vinahouse remix)" (Bích Phương x DJ Anh Khang, 2018)

Bài gốc V-pop ballad Bích Phương phát hành 2018, nhanh chóng thành hit. DJ Anh Khang (và
nhiều DJ khác) remix vinahouse 140 BPM Am minor, drop big-room synth lead, lan toả các quán
bar / club / cafe acoustic VN suốt 2018-2020. Đây là **blueprint** vinahouse modern 138-145 BPM.

## Tham số tổng thể

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Vocal source | Bích Phương — Như Lời Đồn (V-pop original 88 BPM Am ballad) |
| Remix tempo | 140 BPM (pitch up vocal +1 semitone hoặc speed up keep pitch) |
| Key | A minor (giữ key gốc) |
| Time sig | 4/4 strict |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Build 1 – Drop 1 – Verse 2 – Build 2 – Drop 2 – Outro |
| Duration | 4:30 (extended club mix 6:00+) |
| Drop length | 32-bar |

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (16 bar) — 0:00–0:27

- Filtered vocal sample (lowpass 200 Hz → 8 kHz sweep, 8-bar)
- Sub bass enter bar 9
- Atmosphere pad sustained chord Am
- Riser white noise
- Kick filtered out (no 4-on-floor yet, only sub)

### Verse 1 (32 bar) — 0:27–1:20

- **Vocal Bích Phương** original: "Anh không tin em, anh không tin em..."
- Sub bass simple root note 1/8 (Am)
- Soft kick (every 4 bars only)
- Pad sustained chord Am – F – C – G
- Plate verb 1.0s on vocal
- Slap delay 1/4 dotted
- Vocal cut: chỉ giữ verse 1 phrase quan trọng, lặp loop

### Build 1 (16 bar) — 1:20–1:48

- Drum roll snare 1/16 → 1/32 (16-bar build)
- Riser white noise pitch up
- Reverse cymbal sweep
- Kick "drop out" bar 12-15 (silent before drop)
- Vocal sample "anh không tin em" pitched up + reversed reverb
- Filter sweep highpass open

### Drop 1 (32 bar) — 1:48–2:48

- **4-on-floor** kick mọi quarter beat
- **Big-room synth lead**: detuned saw 7-cent + supersaw 5 osc + fifth above stack
- Sub bass + 808 sub (root pitched note Am)
- Side-chain compression bass-to-kick (heavy 35% pump)
- Reese bass on Am (vibrato modulated saw)
- Snare reverb 0.4s gated
- Hi-hat 1/8 off-beat
- **Vocal chopped**: "như lời đồn" (3 syllable) lặp 8× loop pattern
- Plate verb 0.6s on vocal chop
- Chord cycle: Am – F – C – G (drop classic vinahouse minor)

### Verse 2 (16 bar) — 2:48–3:15

- Vocal Bích Phương original verse 2 chunk
- Sub bass continuing
- Soft drum (kick light, no snare)
- Filter half-open partial

### Build 2 (16 bar) — 3:15–3:43

- Same pattern as Build 1
- Vocal sample louder, more echoing

### Drop 2 (32 bar) — 3:43–4:43

- Same as Drop 1 + counter-melody plucked synth (đàn bầu fake)
- Vocal stack 2-part loop
- Layer add: cymbal swell every 8 bar

### Outro (16 bar) — 4:43–4:55

- Filter sweep down (highpass 80 Hz → 8 kHz fade)
- Vocal sample echo trail
- Kick fade
- Sub bass fade

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"Như lời đồn"** ("As they say")
- 3 syllables Vietnamese (Như-lời-đồn)
- Pitch contour: A4 G4 → A4 (fall-rise) — conversational melodic
- **Lặp 8× per drop** = vinahouse signature loop density
- Pitch shift +1 semitone (remix speed up)
- Vocal chopped technique: cut into 3-syllable chunks, lặp pattern
- Ratio hook/drop = 60% (vinahouse hook-heavy)

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −5 LUFS (very loud, club-friendly)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0s on vocal verse, plate 0.6s on vocal chop drop
- **Drums**: 4-on-floor kick + 808 sub layer; snare gated reverb 0.4s; hi-hat 1/8 off-beat
- **Side-chain**: heavy 35% pump bass to kick (signature vinahouse "breathing")
- **Synth lead**: Sylenth1 or Serum supersaw stack 5 osc + detune 7 cent + reverb hall
- **Vocal source**: Bích Phương Như Lời Đồn original cut into chunks; pitch up +1 semitone
- **Filter sweeps**: build-up white noise, outro fade

## Lessons learned

1. **4-on-floor kick STRICT 138-145 BPM** = vinahouse signature; HẠ 130 BPM thành tropical,
   HƠN 150 BPM thành psy-trance — đều KHÔNG còn vinahouse
2. **Vocal source V-pop ballad** ORIGINAL — KHÔNG re-record; chỉ pitch up speed up
3. **Drop 32-bar** standard club mix; 16-bar radio mix
4. **Side-chain compression heavy 35%** = "breathing" effect signature
5. **Big-room synth lead** + **sub bass** + **808 sub** layer foundation drop
6. **Vocal chopped** technique: cut 3-syllable, lặp 8× drop
7. **Filter sweeps** build-up TRƯỚC drop (riser white noise + pitch up) BẮT BUỘC
8. **KHÔNG modulate key** — vinahouse stay 1 key whole song
9. **KHÔNG English code-mix** — vocal source giữ nguyên VN
10. **Audience**: club / cafe acoustic / wedding party VN — production phải LOUD và DANCEABLE
11. **Master −5 LUFS** = hot club sweet-spot; preserves transient dù compress heavy
12. **Đàn bầu / sáo fake** plucked synth (counter-melody drop 2 layer) = "Việt-mix" signature
    differentiate from generic big-room EDM
