# Teardown — "Plastic Love" (Mariya Takeuchi, 1984; arrangement Tatsuro Yamashita)

Bài này là **foundational track** cho Japanese city-pop, viral toàn cầu 2017–2018 nhờ YouTube
algorithm. 4 phút 56 giây, đầy đủ city-pop blueprint: jazz extended chord, walking bass,
Rhodes + Strat, horns 3-part, vocal smooth Mariya. Plastic Love = synonym của "city-pop" trong
internet revival era.

## Tham số tổng thể

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (8 bar) — 0:00–0:18

- **Piano grand intro 4 bar** with jazz chord voicings
- Chord cycle: **Am9 – Dm9 – Bm7♭5 – E7♭9** (i9 – iv9 – iiø7 – V7♭9)
- Bass enters at bar 5 (Fender Jazz Bass walking)
- Drums (kick 1+3, snare 2+4 with ghost note 1/16, hi-hat 1/8 mở-đóng) enter bar 5
- Rhodes Mark II layers in at bar 7
- Strings synth pad (CS-80) light pad

### Verse 1 (16 bar = 2 × 8) — 0:18–0:55

- Chord cycle: **Am7 – Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 – Fmaj7 – Bm7♭5 – E7 – Am7** (i7 – iv7 – ♭VII7 – ♭IIImaj7 – ♭VImaj7 – iiø7 – V7 – i7)
- This is a **jazz minor cycle of fifths** moving down — sophisticated harmonic motion
- Drums settled groove: hi-hat 1/8 mở open on "&" of beat 4 (signature)
- Bass walking 1/8 note between chord roots
- Rhodes comping syncopated chord on "&" of beat 2
- Clean Stratocaster guitar enters bar 9 with cutting funky 1/16 strums
- Vocal Mariya mid-range E4–B4, smooth phrasing relaxed

Lyric pattern (phrase 1, romanized):
```
Totsuzen no kiss ya  netsuretsu na ai no kotoba (12 mora + 12 mora)
Niawanai no  yo  futari wa Plastic Love (8 + 7 mora — Plastic Love anchor)
```

### Chorus (16 bar) — 0:55–1:31

- Chord cycle: **Am7 – Dm7 – Emaj7 – Am7** (i7 – iv7 – ♭VImaj7 chromatic mediant! – i7)
  loop with **chromatic mediant Emaj7** (between E7 and Em7 — jazz substitution)
- Hook "I'm just playing games / I know that's plastic love" with English code-mix
- Vocal climbs F#5–G5 on "Plastic Love" — peak chorus
- Horns 3-part (trumpet + alto sax + tenor sax) enter for chorus, comping behind vocal
- Strings strengthen, bass slightly more active with ghost notes on "&"
- Hi-hat 1/8 fully open

### Instrumental Break (4 bar) — 1:31–1:40

- Piano + bass + drums break
- Brief horn riff fill 4 bar — sets up verse 2

### Verse 2 (16 bar) — 1:40–2:17

- Same chord cycle as verse 1
- Drums build slightly busier — snare ghost notes on 1/16
- Mariya vocal slightly more confident, occasional vocal stack double on phrase ends

### Chorus 2 (16 bar) — 2:17–2:54

- Same as chorus 1 but vocal stack 3-part (lead + 3rd above + 5th below) on hook
- Horns more aggressive

### Solo Section (16 bar) — 2:54–3:31

- **Saxophone alto solo** over verse cycle (jazz minor cycle)
- 16 bar improvised solo — signature city-pop convention
- Strings sustained behind solo
- Drums slightly busier, ride cymbal taps 1/4

### Bridge / C-section (8 bar) — 3:31–3:50

- **Modal shift to A major briefly** (modal mixture)
- Chord: **Amaj7 – F#m7 – Dmaj7 – E7sus** (Imaj7 – vi7 – IVmaj7 – V7sus)
- Vocal Mariya quieter, contemplative
- Horns drop out, Rhodes + Strat clean only
- Bass walking less aggressive

### Final Chorus (16 bar) — 3:50–4:27

- Return to Am7 cycle
- Full arrangement: vocal stack + horns + strings + Rhodes + Strat + bass + drums
- Vocal ad-lib "I'm playing games" repetition
- Hook intensified

### Outro Vamp (16 bar fade) — 4:27–4:56

- Vamp on Am7 – Dm7 – Emaj7 – Am7 cycle
- Vocal scat/ad-lib "doot doo doo" Mariya improvisation
- Horns play riff fills
- Drums groove maintained
- Fade out gradually over 16 bar

## Hook motif analysis

Hook English: **"I'm just playing games / I know that's plastic love"**
- Pitch contour (English part): G5 G5 F#5 E5 D5 / E5 D5 C5 B4 A4 G5
- Peak G5 on "I'm" and "plastic"
- Resolution down to A4 (tonic) on "love"
- English insertion in Japanese verse = signature city-pop bilingual hook

## Chord voicing notes

- **Am9** = A C E G B (root 5 9 13 voicing in piano)
- **Emaj7 in Am context** = E G# B D# (chromatic mediant, "wrong" sounding modal mixture that
  defines Plastic Love mood)
- **iiø7 (Bm7♭5)** = B D F A — half-diminished, jazz minor 2-5-1 cadence
- Bass walking 1/8: A → C → E → G (chord tones up); Dm: D → F → A → C; etc.

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −12 LUFS (vintage analog, NOT loud)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.8s decay on vocals + drums; spring reverb on Strat
- **Tape saturation**: full master through Studer A800 — warm harmonic adds
- **Drum kit**: live recording, ride cymbal stereo wide, snare slightly compressed but not aggressive
- **Bass**: Fender Jazz Bass DI + amp blend; subby low-end cut at 80Hz
- **Vocal**: Neumann U87 close-mic + plate verb dry-wet 70/30; minimal compression (2:1, preserve dynamics)
- **Horns**: 3-part section recorded live in 1 room; each horn has individual mic + room mic blend

## Lessons learned

1. **Jazz extended chord = city-pop signature** — maj7, m7, m9, ø7, dom7 with extensions.
   Without these, sound lapse into "regular pop".
2. **Cycle of fifths jazz minor cycle** in verse — Am7 → Dm7 → G7 → Cmaj7 → Fmaj7 → Bø7 → E7 → Am7
   = sophisticated motion, ear travels through related keys.
3. **Chromatic mediant Emaj7 in Am context** = THE Plastic Love sound. Use modal mixture liberally.
4. **English code-mix in chorus** = signature 80s city-pop bilingual style.
5. **Live band feel essential** — drums, bass, Rhodes, Strat, horns all recorded with humanity.
   Modern programmed kit will lose vibe.
6. **Instrumental SOLO 16 bar** = mandatory, not optional. Sax/guitar solo gives breathing room.
7. **Outro VAMP fade** — bài không có "final cadence", chỉ vamp loop fade out. Conveys "the
   night continues forever" mood.
8. **Master loudness LOW** (−12 LUFS) preserves analog dynamics. Streaming era loudness wars
   destroy this — keep it vintage.
9. **Vocal stack only on hook anchor**, not throughout — verse stays solo lead Mariya intimate.
10. **Hi-hat 1/8 mở-đóng (open-close pattern)** = swing/groove signature. 1/16 closed = wrong feel.
