# Teardown — "Hype Boy" (NewJeans, prod. 250)

Bài này là **blueprint K-pop 4th gen** — UK Garage 2-step revival meets Plastic-Love city-pop bass.
3 phút 0 giây compact, hook density cao (hook lặp 8× chorus), vocal stack 3-part minimal hơn 3rd gen
(BLACKPINK 5-part), KHÔNG modulate. 250 (Hudson Mohawke collaborator) là kiến trúc sư.

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"Hype boy"** (2 syllables Korean-pronounced)
- 2 syllables only — ULTRA-SHORT
- Pitch contour: B4 B4 (varies sometimes B4 → C#5)
- Lặp lại 8× per chorus = high density anchor
- Ratio hook/chorus = 50%+ time spent on hook (very high)

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −7 LUFS (loud nhưng less hyper than 3rd gen K-pop −5 LUFS)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.2s on lead vocal, 0.6s on pluck synth, gentle no aggressive gated
- **Drums**: 2-step UK Garage with shuffle 60% on hi-hat 1/16 — signature
- **Bass**: synth sub (Sub37 deep) layered with electric bass octave-up
- **Tape saturation**: subtle on master bus, 1-2% drive
- **Vinyl crackle**: hidden lo-fi background -40 dB (barely audible but adds vintage feel)
- **Vocal stack**: 3-part max (lead + 3rd + doubled) — minimal compared to 3rd gen 5-part

## Lessons learned

1. **UK Garage 2-step revival** — kick on 1 + 2.5, snare on 2 + 4, hi-hat 1/16 shuffle 60% =
   K-pop 4th gen signature (different from 3rd gen 4-on-floor or trap 1/32)
2. **Plastic Love bass walking** = direct reference to 1984 Mariya Takeuchi city-pop; 4th gen
   borrows heavily from 80s Japanese sound
3. **Jazz extended chord** (maj7, m9, sus4) — verse uses Bmaj7-G#m9-C#m7-F#sus, sophisticated
4. **Hook ULTRA-SHORT** 2 syllables lặp 8× per chorus = ratio hook/chorus 50%+ = high anchor density
5. **NO modulation final chorus** — 4th gen drift away from 3rd gen +1 tradition; layer add thay key change
6. **Vocal stack 3-part max** — minimal compared to 3rd gen 5-part anthemic
7. **Vocal range hẹp E4–F#5** — KHÔNG belt G5+, members focus breath/airy quality intentional
8. **Member rotation** = 5 members chia line; each gets ~12 bars total = signature girl group
9. **Bridge KHÔNG strip to piano** — strip to rhythm only (hi-hat + kick); 4th gen avoids piano-ballad bridge
10. **Master loudness −7 LUFS** — louder than V-pop −9, less than 3rd gen K-pop −5; balanced
