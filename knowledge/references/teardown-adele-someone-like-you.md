# Teardown — "Someone Like You" (Adele, prod. Dan Wilson)

Bài này là **gold standard** cho English pop ballad piano-only minimal. 4 phút 45 giây, **chỉ
piano + vocal** từ đầu đến cuối — không drums, không strings (trong studio version Dan Wilson;
live version có thêm strings). Masterclass cho "ít hơn là nhiều hơn".

## Tham số tổng thể

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Key | A major |
| Tempo | 135 BPM (feels half-time / 67 BPM groove) |
| Time sig | 4/4 (with 6/8 feel due to piano arpeggio) |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Pre – Chorus – Verse 2 – Pre – Chorus – Bridge – Chorus' – Outro |
| Duration | 4:45 |
| Vocal range | G3 – E5 (Adele alto belt; peak E5 in chorus) |

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (4 bar) — 0:00–0:09

- Piano grand only, fingering arpeggio 1/8 note constant
- Chord cycle: **A – C#m – F#m – D** (I – iii – vi – IV)
- Chord change every 1 bar (4 chords / 4 bar)
- Recording space: dry-ish, plate verb 1.5s subtle, predelay 25ms
- Tempo feel: bass note on beat 1 + 3 (piano left hand); right hand 1/8 arpeggio top

### Verse 1 (16 bar = 2 phrase × 8) — 0:09–0:35

- Piano arpeggio continues (consistency = signature)
- Chord cycle repeats: **A – C#m – F#m – D**
- Vocal Adele close-mic, mid-range G3–B4
- 4 dòng câu × 8–10 syllables each, kết cuối dòng peak A4–B4
- KHÔNG drums, KHÔNG strings — vocal + piano only
- Adele's vocal: slight rasp on long sustains, controlled vibrato

Lyric pattern (phrase 1):
```
I heard that you're settled down  (8 syl)
That you found a girl and you're married now  (10 syl)
I heard that your dreams came true  (7 syl)
Guess she gave you things I didn't give to you  (11 syl)
```

### Pre-chorus (4 bar) — 0:35–0:43

- Same piano pattern
- Chord shift: **F#m – D – A – E** (vi – IV – I – V) — pivot toward chorus
- Vocal climb chromatic: B4 → C#5 → D5 (build to chorus peak)
- 2 dòng × 6–8 syl

### Chorus 1 (16 bar) — 0:43–1:09

- Chord cycle: **A – E – F#m – D** (I – V – vi – IV) — axis progression
- Same piano arpeggio (UNCHANGED — texture identical to verse, only chord changes)
- Vocal Adele full belt mode, peak D5–E5 ở "Never mind, I'll find someone like you"
- Vocal stack: lead only + 1 doubled track behind (subtle, not 3-part stack)
- Hook: "Never mind, I'll find someone like you" — 9 syllables, anchor line

Hook pitch contour:
```
Ne- ver mind  I'll  find some- one  like  you
A4  A4   B4   D5   D5  C#5  C#5 B4   A4
```
Peak D5 on "find" and "some-".

### Verse 2 (16 bar) — 1:09–1:36

- Identical texture to verse 1 — piano + vocal only
- Same chord cycle A – C#m – F#m – D
- New lyric, building emotional weight; Adele vocal slightly more weight, pull behind beat

### Pre-chorus + Chorus 2 (20 bar) — 1:36–2:11

- Same as before; vocal slightly more raspy, ad-lib "Don't forget me, I beg" tail end

### Bridge (16 bar = 2 × 8) — 2:11–2:36

- Chord shift introduces new motion: **F#m – D – A – E – F#m – D – A – E**
- (loop of vi – IV – I – V — same as pre-chorus but extended 8 bar)
- Vocal Adele intimate plead: "Nothing compares, no worries or cares"
- Pitch climbs slowly: B4 → D5 → E5 climax on "memories" (E5 sustained 2-second vibrato)
- Piano slightly more aggressive arpeggio (no rest at chord change)
- KHÔNG strings entry yet (in studio version)

### Final Chorus (16 bar + tag, 8 bar) — 2:36–3:18

- Repeat chorus chord cycle A – E – F#m – D
- Adele full belt + ad-lib "I wish nothing but the best for you, too"
- Slight vocal layering enter (lead + 1 octave-down doubled subtle)
- KHÔNG modulate — Adele relies on emotional intensity, not key change
- Tag: "Sometimes it lasts in love, but sometimes it hurts instead" — 8 bar repeat 2× với escalation

### Outro (12 bar) — 3:18–4:45

- Piano arpeggio + vocal "ooh-ooh" tail
- Chord settles: A – F#m – D – A (I – vi – IV – I — final cadence)
- Vocal fade "sometimes it lasts in love" repeated 3× với decreasing volume
- Final piano chord A major sustained

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"Never mind, I'll find someone like you"** (9 syllables)
- Pitch contour: A4 A4 B4 → D5 D5 → C#5 C#5 B4 A4
- Repeat each chorus cycle, anchor word "you" lands on A4 (tonic)
- "find" lands on D5 = subdominant (IV), creating tension
- "some-one like" descends C#5–C#5–B4 = III–III–II downward resolution

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −10 LUFS (NOT loud — preserves dynamic range)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.5–2.0s on vocal + piano (same space, both feel like one room)
- **Compression vocal**: subtle, 2:1, preserve dynamics for Adele's emotional swells
- **Recording**: live piano performance + vocal in same session (Dan Wilson + Adele)
- **Microphone**: vocal SM7B close-mic; piano stereo pair Neumann U87
- **No bus saturation, no master limiter aggressive** — peak protection only

## Lessons learned

1. **Texture stays constant** = vocal does the heavy lifting. Not changing arrangement = focus on vocal performance.
2. **Axis progression I-V-vi-IV** is the most emotional in English pop ballad — used in 100s of hits.
3. **No modulation needed** — Adele doesn't shift key in final chorus. Emotional escalation comes from vocal performance + ad-lib + slight layering.
4. **Bridge = pivot section, not new chord motion** — same vi-IV-I-V loop, but vocal builds.
5. **Chorus hook 9 syllables, peak on subdominant note** — creates yearning that resolves to tonic on final word "you".
6. **Subtle vocal layering** in final chorus only (1 doubled track) — not 4-part anime stack. Less is more.
7. **Master loudness LOW** (−10 LUFS) preserves Adele's dynamic vocal swells. Modern loudness war ruins this.
8. **Live piano+vocal performance** — both recorded together = humanity coupling. Studio splice would lose magic.
