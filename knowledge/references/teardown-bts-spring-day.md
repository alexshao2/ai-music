# Teardown — "Spring Day (봄날)" (BTS, 2017, prod. Pdogg + RM)

Bài này là **K-pop ballad-pop hybrid masterpiece** — kết hợp K-R&B verse + EDM-influenced
chorus + emotional bridge với modulate +1 trước final chorus. RM viết lời literary references
"Snow Country" (Kawabata Yasunari) với ý "winter to spring" metaphor cho separation/reunion.
Pdogg sản xuất với synth + 808 sub + brushed drums hybrid.

## Tham số tổng thể

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Key | B♭ major (verse + chorus 1-2) → B major (final chorus modulate +1 semitone) |
| Tempo | 75 BPM (slow ballad) |
| Time sig | 4/4 |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Pre – Chorus – Verse 2 – Pre – Chorus – Bridge – Final Chorus (modulated) – Outro |
| Duration | 4:35 |
| Vocal range | C3 – C5 (member rotation 7 voices) |

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (8 bar) — 0:00–0:25

- Synth pad swell B♭ major chord enter bar 1-2
- Suga rap whisper "어떻게 하지" (eotteoke haji, "what should I do") bar 3
- Drum machine kick + light snare 2+4 enter bar 5 (75 BPM, half-time feel)
- Vocal "yeah" tag bar 7-8

### Verse 1 (16 bar) — 0:25–1:05

- Chord cycle: **B♭maj7 – Gm9 – E♭maj7 – F7sus4** (Imaj7 – vi9 – IVmaj7 – V7sus) jazz K-R&B
- Lead vocal Jin then V with falsetto delivery
- Drums: 808 kick on 1, snare on 3 (half-time feel), light hi-hat 1/8
- Bass synth deep sub with glide
- Rhodes EP layer chord on beat 1 + 3
- Plate verb 1.2s on vocal

### Pre-chorus (8 bar) — 1:05–1:25

- Chord shift: **Gm – Cm7 – F7sus4 – F7** (vi – ii7 – V7sus – V7)
- Vocal Jimin climb B♭4 → D5 emotional dồn
- Drums slightly busier — kick on 1 + 3, hi-hat opens on "&" of 4
- White noise riser bar 7-8

### Chorus (16 bar) — 1:25–1:55

- Chord cycle: **B♭ – Gm – Cm – F7** (I – vi – ii – V7) classic axis K-pop ballad
- Hook "보고 싶다" (bogo sipda, "I miss you") 4 syllables, lặp 4× per chorus
- Vocal stack 3-part: Jungkook lead + Jimin 3rd above + V doubled
- Drums full kit: 808 kick + brushed snare 2+4 with reverb 0.6s + hi-hat 1/16
- Synth pad fills background
- Plate verb 1.4s on stack

### Verse 2 (16 bar) — 1:55–2:25

- Different members rotate (J-Hope rap section, RM rap section)
- Same chord cycle as verse 1, slightly more drums layered
- Rap delivery 75 BPM half-time pocket — emotional, not aggressive

### Pre-chorus + Chorus 2 (24 bar) — 2:25–3:00

- Same as before; vocal stack add 5th above on bar 12-16; ad-lib by Jungkook

### Bridge (16 bar) — 3:00–3:30

- Chord shift sang **modulation: B♭ → A♭7 → B major** (pivot via A♭7 = bVII7 in B♭ → V/V in B)
- Drums strip down → only piano + bass + light vocal harmonies (members trade)
- Vocal RM emotional spoken-rap "그리워하는데도 그리워하는데도" (longing-but-longing)
- Build back up bar 13-16 with drums + ad-lib

### Final Chorus (16 bar) — 3:30–4:05

- **Modulated +1 semitone to B major** ✓
- Chord cycle: **B – G#m – C#m – F#7** (I – vi – ii – V7) in B major
- Vocal stack 5-part: Jungkook lead + Jimin 3rd + V 5th + Jin doubled + RM/Suga ad-lib
- Drums maintained full kit + tom rolls bar 14
- Snare reverb 1.0s tail (longer for emotional swell)
- Hook "보고 싶다" lặp 4× emotional peak

### Outro (8 bar) — 4:05–4:35

- Drums strip down → Rhodes EP + bass + vocal "보고 싶다" tag fade
- Final cadence: F#7 → B major plagal-influenced
- Vocal "yeah" whisper fade

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"보고 싶다"** (bogo sipda, "I miss you")
- 4 syllables Korean
- Pitch contour: F4 G4 → B♭4 sustained (verse 1-2 chorus)
- Final chorus modulated: G♭4 A♭4 → B4 sustained (transposed +1)
- Lặp lại 4× per chorus = standard K-pop ballad anchor density

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −8 LUFS (typical K-pop ballad, less hyper than idol-pop)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.4s on lead vocal, 1.0s on snare gated subtle, 0.8s on Rhodes
- **Drums**: hybrid — 808 kick electronic + brushed snare acoustic + hi-hat 1/16
- **Bass**: synth sub deep with glide envelope
- **Synth pad**: warm Juno-60 background ambient
- **Modulation pivot**: A♭7 (bVII7 in B♭) → resolves to B major (semi-diminished pivot)
- **Vocal stack**: 5-part max final chorus, 3-part chorus 1-2

## Lessons learned

1. **K-R&B verse + EDM chorus hybrid** — verse jazz Imaj7-vi9-IVmaj7-V7sus K-R&B; chorus classic
   I-vi-ii-V K-pop ballad. Two different worlds in one song.
2. **Modulation +1 BẮT BUỘC final chorus** — K-pop ballad signature; pivot via bVII7 chord (smooth)
3. **Slow tempo 75 BPM với drums full kit** = ballad-pop hybrid; KHÔNG phải pure piano ballad
4. **Member rotation 7 voices** — Jin → V → Jimin → Jungkook → J-Hope → RM → Suga; mỗi member
   ~30 sec; vocal stack escalates 3 → 5 part
5. **Rap section emotional, NOT aggressive** — at 75 BPM rap pocket cảm thấy như spoken-word
6. **Hook 4 syllables Korean lặp 4× per chorus** = ratio hook/chorus 25-30% (lower than 4th gen
   "Hype Boy" 50%, but high than English ballad)
7. **Bridge strip to piano + spoken rap by RM** = literary K-pop ballad signature; emotional pivot
8. **Lyrical literary reference** — "Snow Country" (Kawabata Yasunari novel) = K-pop ballad
   tradition of literary depth (BTS RM-led songs especially)
9. **Plate verb 1.4s + tom rolls final chorus** = emotional escalation typical K-pop ballad
10. **Master −8 LUFS** = K-pop ballad sweet-spot (V-pop ballad −9 to −11; idol-pop −5 to −7)
