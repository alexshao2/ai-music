# Teardown — "Tití Me Preguntó" (Bad Bunny, 2022, from "Un Verano Sin Ti", prod. MAG + La Paciencia)

Bài này là **reggaeton modern masterpiece** kết hợp dembow truyền thống + Afro-Cuban conga +
trap influence + classical guitar break. Bad Bunny + co-producer MAG biến reggaeton từ
party-only thành vehicle storytelling. 4:03 long structure, 3 verse với storytelling về Bad
Bunny's relationships ("Tití me preguntó si tengo muchas novias" = "auntie asked me if I have
many girlfriends"). Conga break giữa bài là moment instrumental signature.

## Tham số tổng thể

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Key | A minor |
| Tempo | 107 BPM |
| Time sig | 4/4 (with 3-3-2 syncopation in bass) |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Coro – Verse 2 – Coro – Conga break – Verse 3 – Coro – Outro |
| Duration | 4:03 |
| Vocal range | C3 – G4 (Bad Bunny baritone with autotune extending up) |

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (8 bar) — 0:00–0:18

- 4 bar instrumental: 808 sub bass + dembow drums + plucked synth (Korg M1)
- Chord cycle: **Am – F – G – Em** (i – bVI – bVII – v) classic minor reggaeton loop
- Hi-hat 1/16 constant; clave 3-2 son rhythm
- Bar 5-8: Bad Bunny ad-lib "ey, ey, Bunny, ey"

### Verse 1 (16 bar) — 0:18–0:55

- Chord cycle continues: **Am – F – G – Em** (4 bar each) lặp 4×
- Vocal Bad Bunny smooth croon with HEAVY autotune (~80% pitch correction)
- Phrasing: tight pocket on dembow snare hits (beats 2.5, 3.5)
- Bass 808 sub deep with glide between chord roots
- Plucked synth riff trên tiếng riêng (Korg M1 melodic line)
- Hi-hat 1/16 constant
- Storytelling lyric "Tití me preguntó si tengo muchas novias..."

### Coro / Chorus 1 (16 bar) — 0:55–1:30

- Same chord cycle: **Am – F – G – Em**
- Hook "Tití me preguntó" 6 syllables Spanish, lặp 2× per chorus + variations
- Vocal stack 2-part: lead Bad Bunny + 3rd above doubled
- Drums maintain dembow pattern
- Plucked synth gets busier (16th note ornaments)
- Plate verb 1.0s on vocal

### Verse 2 (16 bar) — 1:30–2:05

- Different chord variation: **Am – F – C – Em** (i – bVI – bIII – v) brief modal shift
- Bad Bunny continues storytelling with higher emotional intensity
- Drums slightly busier — extra ghost notes on snare 4.5
- Bass 808 walks more — adds octave jumps

### Coro 2 (16 bar) — 2:05–2:35

- Same as Coro 1 with light counter-melody on plucked synth
- Vocal stack 2-part maintained

### Conga break / Mambo (16 bar) — 2:35–3:05

- **THIS IS THE SIGNATURE MOMENT** — Afro-Cuban conga + brass + classical guitar arpeggio
- Drums shift: dembow pattern stays but conga (real, organic, percussive) layered FRONT
- Brass section enters (synth horns) playing chord melody
- Classical Spanish guitar arpeggio in foreground
- Vocal "ay, ay, ay" ad-lib
- Build energy massively; this is the dance section

### Verse 3 (16 bar) — 3:05–3:35

- Return to dembow standard
- New chord cycle slightly different: **Am – Dm – G – C** (i – iv – bVII – bIII)
- Bad Bunny rap/sing hybrid delivery
- Drums maintain pattern

### Coro 3 (8 bar) — 3:35–3:55

- Shortened final chorus
- Vocal stack 3-part max with ad-libs scattered
- Energy peak

### Outro (4 bar) — 3:55–4:03

- Plucked synth riff fade
- Vocal "Bunny, ey" tag
- Final cadence: G → Am

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"Tití me preguntó"** ("Auntie asked me")
- 6 syllables Spanish (Tí-tí-me-pre-gun-tó)
- Pitch contour: A4 G4 → A4 G4 F4 (descending) — conversational melodic
- Lặp lại 2-3× per chorus + variations
- Storytelling hook (NOT repeated 8× like K-pop) — Latin tradition narrative
- Ratio hook/chorus = 25% (typical reggaeton)

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −5.5 LUFS (very loud, club-friendly)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0s on lead vocal, slap delay 1/4 dotted on tail
- **Drums**: dembow constant pattern (kick + snare + hi-hat 1/16 + clave)
- **Conga**: real recorded conga (not sample) — adds organic warmth
- **Bass**: 808 sub deep with glide envelope, sidechain pumped 25% to kick
- **Auto-tune**: heavy on Bad Bunny vocal (~80%), signature sound
- **Plucked synth**: Korg M1 (signature 80s/90s reggaeton sound) + occasional Sub37 melodic
- **Brass section**: synthesized (Spitfire Audio horns library) + real saxophone overdub
- **Classical guitar**: real recorded nylon classical guitar in conga break

## Lessons learned

1. **Dembow rhythm UNCHANGED whole song** — kick on 1, 1.5, 3, 3.75; snare on 2.5, 3.5, 4.5; hi-hat 1/16 constant
2. **Storytelling lyric** = modern reggaeton evolution; Bad Bunny đem reggaeton từ party-only sang narrative
   với personal stories ("Tití auntie asked me about my girlfriends, I lied")
3. **Conga break / Mambo section** giữa bài = Latin tradition; dembow lock vẫn giữ nhưng add organic
   conga + brass + classical guitar = breath of fresh air mid-song
4. **HEAVY autotune** ~80% Bad Bunny signature; modern reggaeton accepts heavy autotune as aesthetic
5. **Master −5.5 LUFS** = reggaeton hot sweet-spot; loud club-friendly
6. **Hook 6 syllables Spanish** lặp 2-3× chorus = Latin storytelling hook density (lower than K-pop 8×)
7. **Auto-tune subtle on harmony, heavy on lead** — production layered choice
8. **Chord motion** classical minor reggaeton i-bVI-bVII-v with brief variations every 16 bar — keeps interest
9. **Ratio Spanish vs English** = 100% Spanish, 0% English — Bad Bunny refuses to release in English (consistent
   marketing strategy)
10. **Real conga + classical guitar** mixed with synthesized brass + 808 = hybrid organic-electronic
    production; modern Latin signature
11. **Form 4:00+ với 3 verse storytelling** = modern reggaeton longer-form structure (vs classic 3:30 tight)
