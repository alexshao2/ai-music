# Teardown — "Yoru ni Kakeru" (YOASOBI, Ayase prod. / Ikura vocal)

Bài này là **đỉnh cao J-pop modern 2019+**. Nguồn gốc novel "Tanatos no Yuwaku" (Mayo Hoshino).
Cấu trúc nhồi 5 motif chính trong 4 phút 22 giây với modulation phức tạp — masterclass cho
"compact storytelling J-pop".

## Tham số tổng thể

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Key | C# minor (verse) → C# minor → modulate D# minor (final sabi) |
| Tempo | 130 BPM |
| Time sig | 4/4 |
| Form | Intro – Verse A – Verse B – Pre-Sabi – Sabi 1 – Verse A' – Verse B' – Pre – Sabi 2 – Bridge (D-section) – Final Sabi (modulated +1) – Outro |
| Duration | 4:22 |
| Vocal range | E4 – G#5 (Ikura belt peak G#5 in modulated final sabi) |

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (4 bar)

- Piano motif 4 bar độc tấu, chord cycle: **C#m – Amaj7 – Bsus4 – B** (i – VI – Vsus – V)
- Drum machine kick 1+3 vào bar 3 với side-chain pump
- Bass synth sub vào bar 4 với riser FX
- Master loud: −6 LUFS already

### Verse A (8 bar) — 0:08–0:23

- Piano chord on beat 1 + 3, hi-hat 1/16 on top half
- Chord: **C#m7 – Bm7 – Amaj7 – G#7** (i7 – vii7 – VI – V/i, secondary dominant để loop)
- Vocal Ikura close-mic, mid-range E4–B4, **fast 1/16 mora delivery** (Anata to mita kono keshiki...)
- Snare nhẹ 2+4 với reverb 0.4s
- 4 dòng × 12–14 mora — kể chuyện girl rủ boy lên sân thượng

### Verse B (8 bar) — 0:23–0:38

- Layer thêm synth pluck (DX7 epiano) chord on beat
- Chord shift sang **F#m7 – G#m7 – Amaj7 – Bsus4–B** (iv – v – VI – Vsus – V)
- Vocal climbing F#4–C#5
- Drum tăng cường: kick 1+3 với 8th note ghost +1.5
- Snare reverb dài hơn 0.6s

### Pre-Sabi (8 bar) — 0:38–0:54

- **Build dồn**: chord change every 1 bar (rất nhanh), riser FX synth
- Chord: **F#m – G#m – Amaj7 – B – C#m – Bm – Amaj7 – G#sus4–G#**
- Vocal climb sequentially up: B4 → C#5 → D#5 → E5 (chromatic build)
- Drum 4-on-floor enter cuối pre, snare flam ở bar 8

### Sabi 1 (16 bar) — 0:54–1:25

- Full kit + 808 sub, gated reverb snare 0.4s, 4-on-floor kick
- Chord cycle: **F#m – G#m – Amaj7 – Bsus4–B** (iv – v – VI – Vsus – V) repeated 4×
- Vocal Ikura belt mode E5 peak, hook "Yoru ni kakeru" ở bar 7 (peak)
- Vocal stack 3-part: lead + 3rd above + octave below
- Strings synth pad enter background
- Hook syllable 6 mora repeated 2× per 4 bar

### Verse A' / B' (16 bar) — 1:25–1:55

- Repeat verse A + B với additional layer (synth lead solo subtle phrase fills)
- Drum pattern slightly busier — hi-hat 1/16 toàn bộ

### Pre-Sabi (8 bar) — 1:55–2:11

- Same as before but with thicker layering

### Sabi 2 (16 bar) — 2:11–2:42

- Same chord cycle, **add layer**: choir pad, doubled vocal, ad-lib "wo-oh" at bar 8

### Bridge / D-section (16 bar) — 2:42–3:13

- **Half-time feel** (kick 1, snare 3 only)
- Chord shift: **Amaj7 – G#m7 – F#m7 – Bsus4** (VI – v – iv – V) — different motion
- Strip xuống piano + vocal + sub bass; drums sparse
- Vocal Ikura quiet mid-range, almost spoken
- Bar 9–16: build back up — drums return, vocal climb chromatic

### Pre-Sabi (4 bar transition) — 3:13–3:21

- Compressed pre-sabi 4 bar only; intense riser; modulation pivot

### Final Sabi (modulated +1 semitone, 16 bar) — 3:21–3:52

- **Key shift to D minor** (was C# minor) — semitone up
- Chord cycle now: **Gm – Am – Bbmaj7 – Csus4–C** (iv – v – VI – Vsus – V) in Dm
- Tom rolls intro, vocal Ikura peak G#5 (originally G5 in old key)
- Vocal stack 4-part now (lead + 3rd + 5th + octave-down + ad-lib)
- Choir pad full, layers maximum
- Hook repeated 4× with intensity escalation each repeat

### Outro (8 bar) — 3:52–4:22

- Piano chord cycle (i – VI – Vsus – V) returns simple
- Vocal "wo-oh" trailing
- Final cadence: D minor → Bb major → A → Dm (Picardy half resolve)
- Drum machine fade out

## Hook motif analysis

```
Yoru ni kakeru
夜に駆ける (yo-ru-ni-ka-ke-ru, 6 mora)
```
- 6 mora distributed over 4 beats (bar 1–2)
- Pitch contour: B4–B4–C#5–B4–E5–E5 (rise to peak on "ke" + sustain "ru")
- Repeat at bar 5–6 với same pitch (anchor)
- Every 4-bar phrase ends với hook = signature

## Production notes

- Master loudness: −6 LUFS (very loud, modern J-pop standard)
- Reverb: plate 1.4s on lead vocal, 0.4s gated on snare
- Side-chain compression aggressive cho synth bass + pad pump theo kick
- Tape saturation across master subtle
- Vocal layered 3–5 part across sabi sections

## Lessons learned

1. **5 motif rule**: J-pop modern bài thường có 5 motif (verse A, verse B, pre, sabi, bridge)
   — không như V-pop ballad 3 motif (verse, pre, chorus)
2. **Modulate +1 BẮT BUỘC trước final sabi** — đây là chữ ký J-pop
3. **Chord change tăng tốc dồn**: verse 4-bar/chord → pre 1-bar/chord → sabi 1-bar/chord
4. **Hook ≤6 mora repeated 2× per 4-bar phrase** — anchor cực mạnh
5. **Bridge half-time** strip-down rồi build back trong cùng 16 bar = drama economy
6. **Vocal stack escalation**: sabi 1 (3-part) → sabi 2 (3-part + ad-lib) → final sabi (5-part full)
7. **Hi-hat 1/16 toàn bộ verse** = drives groove tinh tế; không phải straight 1/8
