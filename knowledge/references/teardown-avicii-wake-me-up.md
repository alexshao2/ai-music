# Teardown — "Wake Me Up" (Avicii, 2013, ft. Aloe Blacc)

"Wake Me Up" là **revolutionary track** kết hợp folk acoustic (banjo, country vocal Aloe Blacc)
với big-room EDM 4-on-floor drop. Released 2013, phá vỡ ranh giới EDM-country, tạo ra
**folktronica** subgenre. Avicii (Tim Bergling) production: tempo 124 BPM, B minor, hook vocal
"So wake me up when it's all over", drop synth + banjo hybrid. Một trong những bài EDM-pop
crossover thành công nhất thập kỷ 2010s.

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"So wake me up when it's all over"**
- 8 syllables English
- Pitch contour: D4 D4 G4 D4 → G4 → A4 (rising emotional)
- **Lặp 4× per chorus** → **lặp 4× per drop** (vocal cut "wake me up" 3-syl chunk)
- Rhyme: "all over" / "wiser, older" — classic AABB
- Theme: universal "growing up" / "waiting for life" — works for festival audience

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −5 to −7 LUFS (festival club-loud)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.4s on vocal, plate 0.6s on vocal cut drop
- **Drums**: 4-on-floor kick + 808 sub + snare gated 0.4s + hi-hat 1/8 off-beat
- **Side-chain**: heavy 30% pump bass to kick (signature EDM "breathing")
- **Synth lead**: Sylenth1 or Spire supersaw stack 5 osc + detune 7 cent + reverb hall +
  banjo arp layer (Kontakt sample of real banjo)
- **Acoustic guitar**: real recorded acoustic guitar (Martin or Taylor) Travis pick fingerstyle
- **Banjo**: real recorded 5-string banjo, picked 1/16 forward roll
- **Vocal**: Aloe Blacc real warm tone, light pitch correction (50% retune speed Auto-Tune
  Pro Classic), plate 1.4s

## Lessons learned

1. **Tempo 124 BPM** = sweet-spot folk-EDM hybrid; sub-130 BPM for vocal-driven crossover
2. **Acoustic guitar fingerpicking + banjo** verse/chorus = folktronica signature
   (KHÔNG nghe được ở pure EDM)
3. **4-on-floor + sub bass + supersaw lead** drop = big-room EDM standard
4. **Hybrid drop**: banjo continuing IN drop (NOT just synth) = folktronica genius
5. **Vocal Aloe Blacc** warm soul/country tone (NOT EDM topline female sample) = differentiator
6. **Hook 8-syllable lặp 4× chorus** + **vocal cut "wake me up" 3-syl lặp 4× drop** =
   density layered approach
7. **Universal theme** "growing up" works for festival audience worldwide
8. **English vocal** với accent neutral (Aloe Blacc) — international appeal
9. **Master −5 to −7 LUFS** = festival club sweet-spot; preserves transient
10. **Drop 16-bar standard** + **3 drops total** (vs typical 2) = more dance density
11. **Chord cycle Bm-G-D-A** anthemic minor i-bVI-bIII-bVII = festival anthem
    formula proven dozens of EDM hits
12. **Bridge stripped-down** "wake me up + acoustic guitar only" = tension reset before
    final drop = signature dynamic arc
13. **Folktronica subgenre established**: Avicii proved EDM + acoustic instruments compatible,
    inspired Marshmello "Happier", Kygo tropical house, Calvin Harris "Slide" with banjo, etc.
