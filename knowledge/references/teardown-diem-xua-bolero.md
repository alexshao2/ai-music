# Teardown — "Diễm Xưa" (Trịnh Công Sơn, 1960; iconic versions: Khánh Ly 1969, Lệ Quyên 2010s)

"Diễm Xưa" là bài hát mang tính biểu tượng nhất của Trịnh Công Sơn — sáng tác 1960 cho
"Diễm" (Bích Diễm), người con gái Huế nhạc sĩ thầm yêu. Bài này định hình **bolero VN**
hiện đại — slow 70 BPM, organ + classical guitar arpeggio, vocal vibrato sâu, lyric kể chuyện
mưa Huế / cố hương / tình cũ. Khánh Ly version 1969 là chuẩn vintage; Lệ Quyên 2010s là
modern bolero revival.

## Tham số tổng thể

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Original key | C major (Khánh Ly version giữ); Lệ Quyên version chuyển E♭ major (vocal range hơi cao hơn) |
| Tempo | 68-72 BPM (slow ballad bolero) |
| Time sig | 4/4 standard bolero (NOT 3/4 waltz) |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Verse 2 – Chorus – Verse 3 – Chorus – Bridge instrumental – Final Chorus – Outro |
| Duration | 4:30 (Khánh Ly version) – 5:00 (Lệ Quyên modern with extended bridge) |

## Section-by-section breakdown

### Intro (8 bar) — 0:00–0:32

- Hammond organ sustained chord C major
- Classical guitar arpeggio fingerpicking 1/8 (Travis pick: thumb bass + index/middle treble)
- Brushed drums very light kick beat 1, snare brush sweep
- Bass acoustic upright (or contrabass) walking
- No vocal yet, instrumental tone-setting
- Plate verb 1.4s on guitar

### Verse 1 (16 bar) — 0:32–1:35

- "Mưa vẫn mưa bay trên tầng tháp cổ / Dài tay em mấy thuở mắt xanh xao..."
- Vocal entry intimate close-mic, vibrato medium 4-5 Hz
- Hammond organ pad sustained chord cycle: C – Am – Dm – G7 – C – F – G7 – C
  (I-vi-ii-V7-I-IV-V7-I "Bolero turnaround")
- Classical guitar arpeggio continuous
- Bass walking quarter notes
- Brushed drums very subtle (kick beat 1 + 3 only, snare brush off-beat)
- Plate verb 1.6s on vocal
- Slap delay 1/4 (subtle)

### Verse 2 (16 bar) — 1:35–2:38

- "Nghe lá thu mưa reo mòn gót nhỏ / Đường dài hun hút cho mắt thêm sâu..."
- Vocal continues, slightly more intensity
- Add light string section (violin + viola) sustained pad chord
- Same chord progression
- Vocal vibrato slightly wider (5-6 Hz)

### Chorus (16 bar) — 2:38–3:25

- "Chiều nay còn mưa sao em không lại / Nhỡ mai trong cơn đau vùi..."
- Vocal stack 2-part light (lead + 3rd above subtle)
- Strings fuller (2nd violin + viola)
- Accordion (signature Latin/bolero element) playing chord melody
- Drums slightly louder brushed
- Bass walking more active
- Plate verb 1.8s
- Modulate from C major chord cycle to slight emotional pivot via secondary dominant
  (C – E7 – Am – F – G7 – C)

### Verse 3 (16 bar) — 3:25–4:15

- "Làm sao có nhau hằn lên nỗi đau / Bước chân em xin về mau..."
- Vocal hồi tưởng tone, register slightly lower
- Same chord cycle as verse 1
- Stripped-down: organ + guitar + vocal only (no drums)

### Bridge / Instrumental break (16 bar) — 4:15–5:00

- **GUITAR LEAD SOLO** classical Spanish style fingerstyle 16 bar (Lệ Quyên modern version)
- Drums lock pattern, bass walking, organ sustained
- Vocal silent

### Final Chorus (16 bar) — 5:00–5:48

- Vocal full belt, vibrato max (6 Hz wide)
- All elements: organ + guitar + bass + drums + accordion + strings + tom rolls subtle
- Vocal stack 3-part chorus harmonization
- Plate verb 2.0s

### Outro (8 bar) — 5:48–6:00

- Fade with guitar arpeggio + organ sustained final chord
- Bass long note C
- Vocal "ah" sustained final note vibrato

## Hook motif analysis

Hook line: **"Mưa vẫn mưa bay trên tầng tháp cổ"** ("Rain still falls on the ancient tower")
- 9 syllables Vietnamese
- Pitch contour: G4 G4 G4 A4 G4 F4 E4 D4 C4 (descending stepwise)
- **Lặp 1× per verse** (NOT density-heavy K-pop style); appears 1× chorus + 1× final chorus
- Rhyme tail: "cổ" / "xao" / "lại" assonant rhyme classic Vietnamese poetry
- Tone: thanh sắc + ngang + huyền (bằng-trắc balanced)

## Production notes

- **Master loudness**: −10 to −12 LUFS (warm vintage, NOT loudness war)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.6-2.0s on vocal (escalating verse → chorus → final)
- **Tape saturation**: subtle 1-2% drive on master (analog warmth)
- **Drums**: brushed (NOT stick); kick very subtle beat 1 + 3 only; snare brush sweep
- **Vocal**: NO autotune, vibrato preserved natural
- **Stereo**: piano/guitar L+R wide (40/40); strings hall L80%/R80%; bass mono center;
  vocal mono center
- **Instruments real**: Hammond organ thật (Leslie speaker rotating cabinet); classical
  guitar real recorded; accordion real; string section real (or sampled VSL/Spitfire)

## Lessons learned

1. **Tempo slow 68-72 BPM** = bolero VN sweet-spot; HẠ <60 BPM thành "lento" khó dance;
   HƠN 90 BPM thành V-pop ballad không còn bolero
2. **4/4 standard** (NOT 3/4 waltz) — phổ biến đa số bolero VN modern
3. **Vocal vibrato sâu 4-6 Hz** = bolero signature; KHÔNG autotune
4. **Hammond organ + classical guitar fingerpick** = foundation harmonic
5. **Bridge GUITAR LEAD SOLO 16-bar** = bolero signature instrumental break (Lệ Quyên
   modern adds this)
6. **Brushed drums** (NOT stick) — softer texture
7. **Storytelling lyric VN** với cliché bolero (mưa, đường, tóc, mắt) NHƯNG poetic
   transformation Trịnh Công Sơn (Diễm Xưa = "ancient Beautiful One")
8. **Pure VN lyric** — KHÔNG English code-mix
9. **Pronoun "em" / "anh"** + định danh ("Diễm") lyrically anchor
10. **Chord cycle ii-V-I cadence** + secondary dominant E7 chorus emotional pivot
11. **Master −10 to −12 LUFS** preserves dynamics + warmth (NOT loudness war pop)
12. **Lệ Quyên modern revival** version uses extended bridge instrumental + slight modulate
    to E♭ major final chorus → modern bolero update preserved tradition
13. **Khánh Ly 1969 version** stays classical: smaller ensemble (organ + guitar + bass +
    light brush drum), more intimate, no string section — proves bolero foundation needs
    only organ + guitar + vocal
