# English Rhyme Techniques & Prosody

Tiếng Anh là **stress-timed language** (khác tiếng Việt syllable-timed, khác tiếng Nhật mora-timed).
Điều này ảnh hưởng trực tiếp đến melody: stressed syllable CẦN PHẢI rơi vào strong beat (1, 3).
Rhyme trong lyric English rất quan trọng — hook memorable = rhyme scheme tốt.

## 1. Stress-timed = stressed syllable trên strong beat

```
ANYthing you WANT, I'll BE your LIGHT
  ↑         ↑       ↑      ↑
  beat 1    beat 2  beat 3 beat 4
```

**Quy tắc 1**: stressed syllable (chữ hoa) PHẢI rơi trên downbeat (1, 2, 3, 4) hoặc strong off-beat (1.5, 3.5).
**Quy tắc 2**: unstressed syllable có thể rơi ở weak position (pick-up, passing tone).

✅ **Pass**: "I WANT to HOLD your HAND" → stress trên beat
❌ **Fail**: "i WANT to hold YOUR hand" → "your" stressed ở wrong beat = prosody mismatch

## 2. Rhyme taxonomy (từ cơ bản → nâng cao)

### 2.1. Perfect rhyme

Vowel + consonant cuối giống hoàn toàn:
- love / above / dove
- time / rhyme / crime
- heart / apart / start

Dùng: hook anchor, cuối mỗi couplet. **Quá dễ đoán nếu dùng mọi câu → nhàm.**

### 2.2. Slant rhyme (near rhyme)

Vowel tương đồng, consonant cuối gần:
- home / alone (ōm / ōn)
- love / enough (ʌv / ʌf)
- rain / game (eɪn / eɪm)
- light / life (aɪt / aɪf)

Dùng: verse, khi muốn sophisticated + avoid predictability. Adele / Sam Smith thường dùng.

### 2.3. Internal rhyme

Rhyme GIỮA câu (không phải cuối câu):
```
I HEARD that you're settled DOWN (heard internal, down end)
That you FOUND a girl and you're MARRIED now (found/married internal)
```

Dùng: tăng density rhythmic; hip-hop storytelling + Adele verse.

### 2.4. Multi-syllabic rhyme

Rhyme 2+ syllables cùng pattern:
- "never mind" / "left behind" (2-syllable)
- "telephone" / "all alone" (3-syllable)
- "contemplating" / "always waiting" (4-syllable)
- "unbelievable" / "inconceivable" (5-syllable, hip-hop)

Dùng: hip-hop dense, chorus emphasis. **Kendrick/Eminem signature.**

### 2.5. Assonance rhyme

Chỉ vowel sound giống, consonant khác:
- hate / face / take / rain (all /eɪ/ vowel)
- broke / know / soul / home (all /oʊ/ vowel)
- time / light / ride / why (all /aɪ/ vowel)

Dùng: tạo harmonic flow mà không obvious rhyme.

### 2.6. Consonance rhyme

Consonant cuối giống, vowel khác:
- back / book / luck (all /k/ ending)
- love / live / leave (all /v/ ending)

## 3. Rhyme scheme cho từng genre

### Pop Ballad (Adele / Sam Smith)

Pattern: **AABB** hoặc **ABAB** đơn giản, with slant rhyme xen kẽ perfect:

```
[A] I heard that you're settled down  (down)
[B] That you found a girl and you're married now  (now)
[A] I heard that your dreams came true  (true)
[B] Guess she gave you things I didn't give to you  (you)
```

now/you = slant rhyme (aʊ / u:). Adele dùng slant thường xuyên để tránh singsong predictable.

**Quy tắc**: pop ballad ≤ 4 perfect rhyme pairs per verse; xen kẽ slant; cuối câu KHÔNG BẮT BUỘC rhyme mỗi dòng.

### Hip-Hop Storytelling (Kendrick / J. Cole)

Pattern: **AABB** tight, internal rhyme dense, multi-syllabic 2–4 syl:

```
[A] I was SCREAMIN' at my MAMA, she was SCREAMIN' at me  (me)
[A] I was DREAMIN' 'bout the DRAMA, she was DREAMIN' to be free  (free)
[B] And I COULDN'T take the PAIN, yeah, I COULDN'T face the game  (game)
[B] 'Cause every TIME I looked AROUND, everything was still the same  (same)
```

- Internal: SCREAMIN' / DREAMIN'; MAMA / DRAMA; PAIN / GAME — multi-syllabic
- End: me / free / game / same = AABB + assonance (ee / ee / eɪm / eɪm)

**Quy tắc**: ≥ 2 internal rhymes per bar, multi-syllabic endpoint; syllable count 8–12/bar.

### Bedroom Pop (Clairo / Boy Pablo)

Pattern: loose, sometimes NO rhyme, conversational:

```
Walkin' with you at the end of summer
I don't need to hold your hand
But I want to
And I think you want to too
```

- "summer" doesn't rhyme with "hand"; "to" / "too" subtle identical
- Bedroom pop trades density for authenticity. **Slant > perfect.**

### Synthwave (The Weeknd / Dua Lipa)

Pattern: **AABB** tight, short punchy lines:

```
[A] I said, ooh, I'm blinded by the lights  (lights)
[A] No I can't sleep until I feel your touch  (touch)
[B] I said, ooh, I'm drowning in the night  (night)
[B] Oh when I'm like this you're the one I trust  (trust)
```

lights / night = slant (aɪts / aɪt); touch / trust = consonance (tʌtʃ / trʌst). Clean, danceable.

## 4. Syllable count guidelines per genre

| Genre | Verse syllables/line | Chorus syllables/line | Hook syllables |
|--|--|--|--|
| Pop Ballad | 8–12 | 6–10 | 6–9 |
| Bedroom Pop | 6–10 | 5–8 | 4–7 |
| Hip-Hop | 10–14 (rap fast) | 6–10 | 4–8 |
| Synthwave | 8–12 | 6–10 | 5–8 |

## 5. Worked examples: GOOD vs BAD

### Example 1: Pop Ballad chorus

✅ **GOOD**:
```
Never mind, I'll find someone like you  (9 syl)
I wish nothing but the best for you, too  (10 syl)
Don't forget me, I beg  (5 syl)
I'll remember you said  (5 syl)
```
Rhyme: you/too = perfect; beg/said = slant. Syllable count balanced.

❌ **BAD**:
```
I will never forget the way you used to look at me  (14 syl — too long for ballad chorus)
And I hope that wherever you are you're doing well  (13 syl — no rhyme target)
Sometimes I cry  (3 syl — too short)
When I remember  (4 syl — no rhyme with above)
```
Lý do sai: syllable count uneven (14, 13, 3, 4); no rhyme pattern; câu đầu quá dài cho chorus.

### Example 2: Hip-Hop verse

✅ **GOOD**:
```
Mama told me there would come a day  (9 syl, end: day)
When I'd have to find my own way  (8 syl, end: way — perfect rhyme)
Walkin' down the block, I stopped to pray  (9 syl, end: pray — perfect rhyme)
Now I'm standing here but what can I say  (10 syl, end: say — perfect rhyme)
```
Internal: "told / come"; "find / own"; "block / stopped"; multi-syllabic: "a day / a way / to pray / I say".

❌ **BAD**:
```
My mother informed me that eventually
There would arrive a moment when I'd need to find independence
Walking along the pavement I paused
Now I am standing here
```
Lý do sai: formal language ("informed", "eventually"); no rhyme; syllable count chaotic (13, 14, 9, 6).

### Example 3: Bedroom Pop verse

✅ **GOOD**:
```
Tuesday afternoon, you called me back  (8 syl)
Said you left your sweater at my place  (8 syl)
I held it close, still smells like you  (7 syl)
And I don't know what I'm gonna do  (8 syl)
```
Slant: back/place (aeK/eɪs); you/do (u:/u:). Concrete details: Tuesday, sweater, smell.

❌ **BAD**:
```
Today you contacted me  (7 syl)
To retrieve your garment  (5 syl — "garment"? conversational song?)
I was sad because I miss you  (8 syl)
My heart is broken  (4 syl — cliché, no concrete detail)
```
Lý do sai: "contacted", "retrieve", "garment" = formal, not conversational; "heart is broken" = cliché.

## 7. Self-check checklist cho Lyricist (English)

1. ☐ Stressed syllables rơi trên strong beat (1, 3)?
2. ☐ Verse syllable count consistent (±2 syl giữa các dòng)?
3. ☐ Chorus hook ≤10 syllables?
4. ☐ Rhyme scheme rõ ràng (AABB, ABAB, hoặc intentional free)?
5. ☐ Ít nhất 1 slant rhyme per verse (tránh singsong predictable)?
6. ☐ Tránh cliché ("broken heart", "tears falling")?
7. ☐ Có concrete details (tên đồ vật, thời gian cụ thể, mùi, vị)?
8. ☐ Conversational tone (viết như nói, không formal "acquire"/"retrieve")?
9. ☐ Multi-syllabic rhyme ít nhất 1× trong bài (nếu hip-hop: mỗi bar)?
10. ☐ Hook repeatable khi hát — phonetically smooth (no consonant cluster khó)?
