# Country — Storytelling Lyric Pattern

Country lyric tradition khác xa K-pop / pop EDM: SPECIFIC people, places, things; brand names
real; concrete imagery (truck, road, beer, mom's kitchen). Modern country still maintains this
tradition (Luke Combs, Morgan Wallen, Kacey Musgraves). Đây là blueprint cho Lyricist khi
compose country.

## 1. SPECIFIC > GENERIC (rule #1)

Country = chi tiết cụ thể. Tránh abstract / generic.

| Generic (BAD) | Specific (GOOD) |
|--|--|
| "I drive my car" | "I'm rolling down Highway 65 in my Chevy Silverado" |
| "I drink some beer" | "Tossing back another Coors Light by the bonfire" |
| "She works in a small town" | "She works the night shift at Waffle House on Main Street" |
| "I love my home" | "Daddy's farm out past the railroad tracks where the corn grows tall" |
| "She wore pretty jeans" | "She wore those Wrangler jeans that fit just right" |
| "We danced together" | "We slow-danced in the bed of my truck under the harvest moon" |
| "I miss my mom" | "Mama's kitchen still smells like apple pie on Sunday mornings" |
| "Saturday night" | "Friday night lights down at Lincoln County stadium" |

## 2. Brand names + locations real

**Trucks**: Chevy Silverado, Ford F-150, Dodge Ram, Chevy 1500, Tahoe
**Cars**: Mustang, Camaro, Cadillac, Lincoln Town Car
**Beer**: Coors Light, Bud Light, Miller High Life, PBR, Yuengling, Modelo
**Whiskey**: Jack Daniel's, Jim Beam, Jameson, Crown Royal, Tennessee whiskey, bourbon
**Clothing**: Wrangler, Levi's, Carhartt, Justin boots, Stetson hat, John Deere cap
**Locations**: Tennessee, Texas, Mississippi, Alabama, Kentucky, Georgia, Oklahoma
**Cities**: Nashville, Memphis, Austin, Knoxville, Jackson, Tupelo
**Streets**: Main Street, Broadway, Highway 65, Route 40, dirt roads
**Stores**: Walmart, Walgreens, gas station (BP, Shell, Marathon), 7-Eleven
**Food**: BBQ, biscuits and gravy, sweet tea, fried chicken, apple pie, cornbread
**Brand TV/movie/song refs**: ESPN, Friday night football, "Hank Williams song", "old George Strait album"

## 3. Storytelling structure — Verse-Verse-Chorus 3-act

### Act 1: Verse 1 — Setup (who, what, where, when)

```
[Beautiful Crazy — Luke Combs verse 1]
"She's pretty crazy, sing a song long as the radio's on
She's pretty driving fast, has the rear-view in her grasp
And she's pretty mad at me, in this Friday night street fight
Yeah, she's pretty hard to handle, she's pretty out of bounds
She's pretty crazy, my favorite is her cra-zy"
```

→ Thiết lập: nhân vật cô gái (cô ấy), tính cách "crazy", bối cảnh Friday night.

### Act 2: Verse 2 — Conflict / Develop

```
[Verse 2 develops the relationship — they fight, they make up]
```

→ Thêm thông tin: relationship history, conflict, tension.

### Act 3: Chorus → Final Chorus — Resolution / Reflection

```
"And I love it, I can't help it
She's just my kind of crazy
Beautiful crazy"
```

→ Resolution: dù cô ấy "crazy", nhân vật yêu cô ấy.

## 4. Rhyme scheme country

### AABB (most common)

```
Whiskey on the table, Friday night so bright (A)
Cool breeze on the porch, stars shining tonight (A)
Pickup truck out back, Chevrolet kind of cool (B)
And momma's homemade apple pie waiting on the stool (B)
```

### AABA classic

```
She wore that smile like a Sunday dress (A)
Made me feel like I was second best (A)
Now she's gone and I'm here alone (B)
Wishing things had turned out the best (A)
```

### Slant rhyme (acceptable in country)

- "road" / "showed" / "low" — assonant slant
- "right" / "tonight" / "light" — perfect
- "town" / "down" / "around" — perfect

## 5. Themes — country canon

| Theme | Sub-themes |
|--|--|
| Love | First love, falling apart, trying again, second chances, longtime |
| Heartbreak | Drinking after breakup, missing someone, can't forget, regret |
| Home / Family | Mama, daddy, growing up, hometown, leaving, returning |
| Drinking | Bar life, whiskey, beer, neon sign, jukebox, hangovers |
| Work | Blue-collar, farming, factory, oilfield, trucking, military |
| Country pride | Small town life, rural USA, flag, faith, freedom, fishing |
| Truck culture | Pickups, dirt roads, off-roading, tailgates, rural drives |
| Religion / faith | Sunday church, prayers, gospel, Jesus (modern: subtle vs explicit) |
| Patriotism / military | Soldiers, veterans, flag, hometown heroes |
| Summer nights | Bonfires, swimming, fishing, county fairs, fireflies, moonshine |

## 6. Vocabulary "twang" indicator

Country accent / vocabulary — use these to set tone:

- "y'all" (you all)
- "ain't" (am not / is not)
- "gonna" (going to)
- "wanna" (want to)
- "lookin'" / "drivin'" / "talkin'" (drop -g)
- "fixin' to" (about to)
- "darlin'" (darling)
- "honey" / "babe" / "sugar" (terms of endearment)
- "hell yeah", "hell no"
- "buddy", "brother"
- "ma'am", "sir" (Southern politeness)
- Possessive "ma" (mama) / "pa" (papa)
- Lyric should sound natural-spoken, NOT formal

## 7. Worked example — Original country composition

### Brief: Modern country, theme "first love at county fair", 110 BPM G major.

**Verse 1**:
```
First time I saw her was at the Lincoln County Fair       (12 syl, country specific)
Eating funnel cake with cotton candy in her hair          (12 syl)
Wrangler jeans and a denim jacket two sizes too small     (13 syl, brand name)
Dancing to a Garth Brooks song like she was nine feet tall (13 syl, song ref)
```

**Chorus**:
```
And it was you, just you, that summer night so true       (11 syl)
With the carnival lights catching in your eyes blue        (11 syl)
We rode the Ferris wheel until the ride was through       (12 syl)
First love at the Lincoln County Fair, just me and you    (13 syl)
```

**Verse 2** (develops):
```
Now it's been ten years and the fair don't come no more   (11 syl, time shift)
But every time I drive past Highway 24                     (11 syl, location)
I think about that summer when we first held hands         (12 syl)
And I wonder where you are now, my fair-grounds girl        (12 syl)
```

✅ **Pass**: SPECIFIC details (Lincoln County Fair, funnel cake, Wrangler jeans, Garth Brooks
song, Highway 24), vần chân -air/-eau-ate/-our-ow consistent, theme first love + nostalgia
country canon, "twang" feel via "don't come no more".

## 8. Cliché country CẦN TRÁNH (overused)

| Cliché | Lý do tránh | Thay thế |
|--|--|--|
| "Country girl in cut-off jeans on a tailgate" | Bro-country trope overused | Specific girl with name + specific situation |
| "Cold beer on a hot day" | Generic | "Half-empty Bud Light warming up in the truck cab" |
| "Dirt road in my Chevy truck" | Bro-country baseline | "Old dirt road past Larson's farm where my grandpa used to drive" |
| "Whiskey washing away the pain" | Direct sad-country cliché | "Bottom of the bottle still won't bring her back" |
| "Mama always told me" | Generic mom advice | Specific mom advice with concrete detail |
| "Praying every night" | Generic faith | Specific church / specific prayer |
| "Red, white, and blue" generic patriotism | Empty | Specific story (uncle in service, hometown veteran) |
| "Dancing under the moon" | Generic | "Dancing in your kitchen at 2 AM in your daddy's old Eagles t-shirt" |

## 9. Self-check checklist cho Lyricist (Country)

1. ☐ Verse 1 có specific people + places + things (NOT generic)?
2. ☐ Brand names real (Chevy, Coors, Wrangler, Jack Daniel's) ít nhất 1× per song?
3. ☐ Locations specific (Tennessee, Highway 65, Main Street, county/town name)?
4. ☐ Storytelling 3-act structure (setup → conflict → resolution)?
5. ☐ Rhyme AABB hoặc slant rhyme acceptable?
6. ☐ Vocabulary "twang" (y'all, ain't, gonna, droppin' -g) phù hợp scene?
7. ☐ Pronoun consistent (1st-person "I" / "we" / direct address "you")?
8. ☐ Theme phù hợp country canon (love, home, drinking, work, truck, summer)?
9. ☐ Tránh cliché bro-country ("dirt road + Chevy + cut-off jeans + cold beer" full set)?
10. ☐ Concrete imagery ≥5 cụ thể (NOT abstract)?
11. ☐ KHÔNG modern slang non-country ("bestie", "lit", "vibing")?
12. ☐ Verse 12-15 syllable, chorus 8-12 syllable?
