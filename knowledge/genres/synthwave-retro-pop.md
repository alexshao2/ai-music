# Synthwave / Retro-Pop — Cookbook đầy đủ

Synthwave (~2010+) hồi sinh sound 80s với synth analog (Juno, Prophet, DX7), drum gated reverb,
arpeggio Moroder-style, vocal stylized retro. Modern retro-pop (The Weeknd "Blinding Lights",
Dua Lipa "Future Nostalgia") chính là phiên bản mainstream. Daft Punk "Random Access Memories"
là blueprint. Darkwave (The Midnight, FM-84) là phiên bản dark melancholic.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 100–128 BPM. Mainstream retro-pop 110–120 (Blinding Lights 171/2≈86 half-time, but groove feels 120); darkwave 90–110 |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%) — synthwave lock 4/4 4-on-floor |
| Key | Minor dominant ~65% (Am, Dm, Fm, F#m); major cho upbeat (D, F) |
| Vocal range | Nữ E4–F#5 (Dua Lipa); nam C3–G4 với falsetto C5 (The Weeknd) |
| Master loudness | −7 đến −9 LUFS streaming; analog tape feel |
| Texture | Synth lead + Juno pad + sub bass + LinnDrum hoặc Roland TR-909 + occasional clean Strat + heavy chorus FX |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (8–16 bar, arpeggio loop signature)
→ Verse 1 (16)  →  Pre-chorus (8)  →  Chorus (16)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Pre-chorus  →  Chorus
→ Bridge / synth solo (8–16, can be instrumental synth lead)
→ Final Chorus (16, pump)  →  Outro (8–16, arpeggio fade)
```

**Key**: synthwave LOVES instrumental break — synth solo 16 bar trước final chorus là chuẩn.
Mainstream retro-pop (Dua Lipa) skip solo, nhưng vẫn có instrumental hook layer.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Synthwave loop | i bVI bIII bVII | Am F C G — Foundation, "Take On Me" base |
| Darkwave | i v bVI bVII | Am Em F G |
| The Weeknd | I IV vi V | F Bb Dm C ("Blinding Lights" Fm relative) |
| Dua Lipa "Don't Start Now" | Bm A G F#7 | i bVII bVI V (Phrygian feel) |
| Driving | i bVII bVI bVII | Em D C D (Pretender feel) |
| Major retro | I V vi iii IV I | C G Am Em F C — disco influenced |

**Trick**: chord change every 2 bar (slow); arpeggio synth chuyển theo chord; **bass synth + sub
glide between root notes** signature.

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Intro**: arpeggio synth (Juno/Prophet) loop 4 bar lặp 2 lần; gradually thêm pad + drums
- **Verse**: arpeggio + sub bass + drum machine (kick 1+3, snare 2+4 with gated reverb 0.4s, hi-hat 1/8); vocal close
- **Pre-chorus**: synth riser → drum tom roll → snare flam; vocal dồn
- **Chorus**: full synth stack (bass + arpeggio + lead syrup + pad), 4-on-floor kick (mainstream)
  hoặc kick 1+3 (darkwave); vocal stack 3-part with side-chain pump
- **Bridge / solo**: synth lead solo (Juno PWM lead, or DX7 brass) over chord pad — 16 bar
- **Final Chorus**: thêm layer (gated snare doubled, choir pad, BG vocal "ohhh"), pump compress
- **Outro**: arpeggio loop fade với reverb tail dài

## 6. Vocal style

- **Tone**: stylized retro — slight detune, layer with itself octave-down
- **The Weeknd**: smooth falsetto + layer ad-lib; Dua Lipa: deeper alto belt with breath
- **Phrasing**: on-beat lock-tight; less back-phrasing than ballad
- **Stack**: lead + double octave-down + 3rd above chorus + ad-lib
- **Reverb**: plate 1.5s + tape echo 1/4 dotted on ad-lib
- **Effects**: vocoder occasional (Daft Punk style); auto-tune subtle (modern retro)

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: night, neon city, dance floor, love affair, escape, "running away"
- **Imagery**: neon lights, midnight, highway, leather jacket, "starlight", "horizon", "city skyline"
- **Pronoun**: "I", "you", "we" — direct second-person
- **Hook short** 5–8 syllables ("blinding lights", "don't start now", "save your tears")
- **Verse câu 8–12 syllables**, chorus câu 5–10
- **Rhyme**: AABB or ABAB; multi-syllabic in chorus (xem `english-rhyme-techniques.md`)
- **Repetition** in chorus is key — "I said, ooh, I'm blinded by the lights"

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng live drum kit — phải drum machine LinnDrum/TR-909
- Đừng acoustic instrument lead (acoustic guitar lead = wrong genre)
- Đừng modern EDM drop big-room — phải retro 80s feel
- Đừng vocal raw natural — phải có stylization (detune, layer, plate verb)
- Đừng forget arpeggio — synthwave WITHOUT arpeggio synth = không đúng genre
- Đừng tempo 160+ — đó là EDM, không phải synthwave

## 10. Liên hệ persona prompts

- Theorist: i-bVI-bIII-bVII synthwave loop; minor key dominant; chord change every 2 bar
- Composer: arpeggio motif foundation; hook 5–8 syllables repeat; falsetto peak (nam) or alto belt (nữ)
- Lyricist: night/neon/escape theme; second-person address; multi-syllabic rhyme
- Arranger: synthwave stack core (bass + arpeggio + pad + lead); instrumental solo 16 bar bridge
- Producer: −7 đến −9 LUFS; gated reverb snare; tape saturation; reference The Weeknd/Dua Lipa/The Midnight
