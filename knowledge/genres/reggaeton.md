# Reggaeton — Cookbook đầy đủ

Reggaeton (Puerto Rico origin, 1990s+) là dòng dancehall + Latin American hybrid. **Dembow rhythm**
là backbone signature: kick + snare pattern không thay đổi suốt bài, kéo dài từ 1990s đến nay.
Modern reggaeton (Bad Bunny, J Balvin, Karol G) thêm trap influence + EDM production. Streaming
era 2017+ làm reggaeton dominant Latin pop globally.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 90–105 BPM (dembow lock); modern slow 88–95, classic 100–105 |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%) |
| Key | Minor dominant ~75% (Am, Em, Fm, Gm); major cho upbeat (D, F) |
| Vocal range | Spanish belt: nữ A3–E5 (Karol G); nam C3–G4 (Bad Bunny baritone, Anuel AA tenor) |
| Master loudness | −5 đến −7 LUFS (very loud, club-friendly) |
| Texture | Dembow drums + 808 sub + plucked synth + clave + Latin percussion + autotuned vocal |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, dembow loop signature OR Latin sample)
→ Verse 1 (16, vocal-led with dembow pattern)
→ Pre-hook (4–8, build subtle)
→ Hook / Coro (16, hook lặp 4×)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Pre-hook  →  Coro
→ Bridge / Puente (8, beat switch hoặc strip-down)
→ Final Coro (16, layer max)
→ Outro (4–8 fade, vocal tag)
```

**Modern Bad Bunny structure** thường dài hơn (4:00+), với 3 verse và 2 hook chính. Classic
reggaeton ngắn hơn (3:00–3:30) tight.

## 3. Dembow rhythm — backbone signature

```
Kick:  X . . . X . . . X . . . X . . .   (hit 1, 1.5, 3, 3.75)
Snare: . . . X . . X . . . . X . . X .   (hit 2.5, 3.5, 4.5)
Hi-hat: X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X  (1/16 constant)
Clave: . . X . . X . . X . . X . . X .   (3-2 son clave)
```

This pattern **NEVER CHANGES** in classic reggaeton — verse, chorus, bridge all use same dembow.
Energy comes from **layering instruments** + **vocal density** + **filter sweeps**.

## 4. Vòng hợp âm hay

Reggaeton borrow từ Latin pop + R&B:

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Reggaeton minor loop | i bVI bIII bVII | Am F C G — Foundation |
| Latin pop axis | I V vi IV | C G Am F |
| Bad Bunny style | i V/i bVI v | Am E7 F Em (secondary dominant) |
| Modal bachata | i bIII bVII iv | Em G D Am (modal mix) |
| Static loop | i (single chord vamp + sample) | Em pedal + Latin sample |
| Modulation final | ±1 hoặc capo shift | Am → Bm |

**Trick**: chord change every 4 bar (slow); chord inversion + suspended; **Latin minor-to-major
oscillation** trong chorus.

## 5. Phối khí per section

- **Drums**: dembow pattern (kick + snare + hi-hat 1/16) constant; tom rolls + cymbal crash on transitions
- **Bass**: 808 sub with glide; reinforced by reggaeton-style plucked synth bass octave-up
- **Keys**: synth pluck (Korg M1, Sub37); occasional Rhodes for romantic touch
- **Latin percussion**: clave (3-2 son), conga (Bad Bunny "Tití Me Preguntó" — afro-Cuban), shaker
- **Synth**: lead synth pluck or pad; melodic riff that layers over dembow
- **Vocal**: heavy autotune (Bad Bunny signature), doubled in chorus, ad-libs scattered
- **FX**: plate verb 1.0s, slap delay 1/4, vinyl crackle in interludes, filter sweeps on transitions
- **Master**: hot loud −5 to −7 LUFS, sidechain pump aggressive

## 7. Vocal style

- **Tone**: smooth Spanish baritone (Bad Bunny, Maluma); confident female alto (Karol G); rugged (Anuel AA)
- **Phrasing**: tight pocket on dembow snare hit (beat 2.5, 3.5)
- **Auto-tune**: HEAVY (Bad Bunny signature 100% autotune), light (Karol G), variable
- **Stack**: lead solo verse → +octave below pre → +3rd above chorus → ad-lib chorus 2/final
- **Ad-libs**: "wuh", "ay", "ey", "que pasa", "Bunny" tag scattered
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0s, slap delay 1/4 dotted on tail
- **Compression**: heavy 4:1, vocal sit forward in mix

## 8. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: love (sensual, longing, breakup), party, female empowerment (Karol G), introspection (Bad Bunny moodier tracks), money/success
- **Spanish**: pronoun "tú" (you informal), "yo" (I); slang Puerto Rican / Colombian / Spanish vary
- **English code-mix**: 1-2 từ ("baby", "OK", "yeah") rare; reggaeton mostly stays Spanish
- **Hook short** 4–8 syllables Spanish, lặp 4× ("Tití me preguntó", "Bichota", "Mi gente")
- **Verse câu 8–12 syllables**, chorus câu 6–10
- **Rhyme**: Spanish assonant (-ar, -er, -ir verb endings) common; rhymes -ona, -ato, -ina

## 10. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng change dembow pattern mid-song — backbone constant rule
- Đừng tempo > 110 BPM — đó là dancehall hoặc moombahton
- Đừng bỏ clave — Latin percussion essential
- Đừng vocal belt operatic — Spanish reggaeton expect smooth croon hoặc autotuned
- Đừng English code-mix nặng — phải Spanish-dominant
- Đừng skip 808 sub — bass foundation
- Đừng cliché "mi amor te quiero" thẳng — modern reggaeton tinh tế hơn
- Đừng modulate +2 — reggaeton hiếm khi modulate
