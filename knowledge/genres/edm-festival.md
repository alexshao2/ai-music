# EDM Festival / Big-Room

EDM Festival = dòng EDM mainstream phục vụ stage festival lớn (Tomorrowland, Ultra,
EDC). Đại diện: Avicii (folktronica + EDM hybrid), Martin Garrix, Tiësto, Hardwell,
Calvin Harris (pop EDM), Zedd, Alesso, Axwell ^ Ingrosso. Tempo 128 BPM strict, 4-on-floor,
big-room synth lead, drop 16-bar mạnh.

## Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 126–130 BPM (sweet-spot 128 BPM) |
| Key | Minor key dominant (Am, Bm, Em, F#m, Gm) — minor more anthemic feel |
| Time sig | 4/4 strict |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Build 1 – Drop 1 – Verse 2 – Build 2 – Drop 2 – Outro |
| Duration | 3:00–4:30 (radio); 5:30–7:00 (club extended) |
| Drop length | 16-bar standard; 32-bar club |

## Cấu trúc song-form

```
Intro (16 bar) — atmospheric pad + filtered vocal sample
Verse 1 (16-32 bar) — vocal melody clear, sub bass entrance, soft drum
Build 1 (8-16 bar) — drum 1/16 → 1/32 roll, riser white noise pitch up, kick drop bar 4
Drop 1 (16-32 bar) — 4-on-floor + big-room synth lead + sub bass
Verse 2 (16 bar) — vocal continue, may add layer
Build 2 (8-16 bar)
Drop 2 (16-32 bar) — repeat drop, may add counter-melody/topline
Outro (16 bar) — filter sweep down, vocal sample fade
```

## Vòng hợp âm

### Big-room minor (most common)

```
Drop: i – bVI – bIII – bVII (Am – F – C – G) — anthemic minor loop
Or:    i – bVII – bVI – bVII (Am – G – F – G)
```

### Anthemic harmonic minor (epic feel)

```
i – iv – bVI – V (Am – Dm – F – E) — V dominant with leading tone
i – V – bVI – bVII (Am – E – F – G)
```

### Festival pop (Calvin Harris, Avicii pop)

```
I – V – vi – IV (axis) — major key uplifting
vi – IV – I – V (Am – F – C – G) — relative minor start
```

## Drum pattern

**4-on-floor** (universal EDM kick):
```
Kick:  X . . . X . . . X . . . X . . . (every quarter)
Clap:  . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . . (beat 2 + 4)
Snare: . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . . (with clap, layered)
HiHat: . X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X (off-beat 8th)
OpenHat: . . . O . . . O (open every 1.5/3.5)
```

## Phối khí

### Intro

- Atmospheric pad (Sylenth1, Serum) sustained chord
- Filtered vocal sample (lowpass 200 Hz → 8 kHz sweep)
- Sub bass enter bar 8
- Riser low frequency

### Verse

- Vocal melody clear (Sara Sigmundsdottir / Aloe Blacc / Joe Janiak topline style)
- Sub bass simple root note 1/8
- Soft drum (kick light, no snare yet)
- Pad layered

### Build

- Drum roll snare 1/16 → 1/32 (8 bar)
- Riser white noise + reverse cymbal
- Pitch-up sweep filter
- Kick "drop out" bar 4 of build (silent before drop)

### Drop

- **Big-room synth lead** signature: detuned saw 7-cent + supersaw 5 osc + fifth above stack
- Or **plucked synth** (Avicii folktronica): pluck pitched arp + delay
- 4-on-floor kick + sub bass + 808 sub
- Side-chain compression bass to kick (heavy 25-40% pump)
- Reese bass on chord 1 (vibrato modulated saw)
- Snare reverb 0.4s gated

### Outro

- Filter sweep down (HPF 80 Hz → 8 kHz fade)
- Vocal sample echo trail
- Kick fade

## Vocal style

- **Topline vocal**: clear melodic, syllable-controlled
- **Featured vocal** (Aloe Blacc, Bebe Rexha): pop vocal with light autotune
- **Sample chop**: vocal cut into 1-2 syllable chunks, lặp loop in drop
- Reverb plate 1.0s + slap delay 1/4 dotted
- Pitch correction subtle (50% retune speed)
- Vocal range: nữ A3-E5; nam C3-F4

## Lyric idiom

- **English** dominant (international festival audience)
- **Universal themes**: love / freedom / dancing / unity / night / journey
- Hook 4-8 syllable, lặp 4-6× per drop
- **Inspirational tone**: "we are forever / we will rise / never let go"
- Verses storytelling but not too specific — leave room for audience interpretation
- Imagery: night, lights, sky, waves, fire, wings, road, tomorrow
- Avoid V-pop / J-pop specific imagery

## DO / DON'T

**DO**:
- Tempo 128 BPM strict
- Drop 16-bar minimum
- Build-up 8 bar (drum roll + riser + pitch up)
- Side-chain compression bass-to-kick (heavy pump)
- 4-on-floor kick mọi quarter beat
- Snare gated reverb 0.4s
- Synth lead big-room detuned saw + supersaw
- English vocal topline universal theme

**DON'T**:
- KHÔNG tempo dưới 124 BPM (tropical) hoặc trên 132 BPM (psy-trance)
- KHÔNG dùng acoustic instruments thật ở drop (synthesized only)
- KHÔNG modulate key trong drop (1 key whole song)
- KHÔNG dembow rhythm (reggaeton)
- KHÔNG vocal gripping rap/storytelling — vocal phải melodic singable
- KHÔNG drop quá ngắn <16 bar
- KHÔNG English specific cultural reference (festival = international universal)

## Persona linkage

- **Theorist**: tempo 128 BPM strict; key Am/Bm/Em/F#m/Gm minor (anthemic); chord cycle
  i-bVI-bIII-bVII hoặc i-iv-bVI-V harmonic minor; KHÔNG modulate.
- **Composer**: form Intro/V1/Build/Drop/V2/Build/Drop/Outro. Drop 16-bar standard. Hook
  4-8 syllable lặp 4-6× per drop. Phrase 6-10 syllable chorus.
- **Lyricist**: English dominant universal theme love/freedom/night. Hook 4-8 syl ngắn.
  Avoid VN/JP specific imagery. Inspirational tone.
- **Arranger**: 4-on-floor kick, sub bass, side-chain compression, big-room synth lead
  detuned saw + supersaw stack, snare gated reverb 0.4s.
- **Producer**: LUFS −5 to −7 club-loud; sub bass mono; side-chain pump 25-40%; filter
  sweeps build/outro; reverb gated 0.4s on snare; reference Avicii / Martin Garrix /
  Tiësto / Calvin Harris / Hardwell.
