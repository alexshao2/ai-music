# English Pop Ballad — Cookbook đầy đủ

English pop ballad (Adele, Sam Smith, Lewis Capaldi, Birdy, Olivia Rodrigo "drivers license")
là dòng ballad mainstream Anh-Mỹ với piano-led production, vocal mạnh (belt soulful), lyric
direct về tình yêu/chia ly, master loud streaming-friendly.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 60–96 BPM. Slow 60–75 (Adele "Hello"); mid 75–96 (Sam Smith) |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~80%); 6/8 cho rocking ballad (Adele "Someone Like You" verse implied 4/4 nhưng feel 6/8) |
| Key | Major chiếm ~55% (A, F, G, C, Eb); minor cho heartbreak (E, A, F minor) |
| Vocal range | Nữ G3–F5 alto/mezzo (Adele G3–E5); nam B2–G4 (Sam Smith C3–F#4 với falsetto B4) |
| Master loudness | −9 đến −7 LUFS streaming |
| Texture | Piano grand + strings + light drums + bass + occasional electric guitar / acoustic |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, piano riff hoặc strings + vocal hum)
→ Verse 1 (16)  →  Pre-chorus (8)  →  Chorus (16)
→ Verse 2 (8–16, thêm layer)  →  Pre  →  Chorus
→ Bridge (8–16, key change tạm hoặc strip-down)
→ Final Chorus (16, big — modulate +1 OR layer thêm BG, KHÔNG strict modulate như anime)
→ Outro (4–8 bar, piano + vocal tail, fade hoặc final cadence)
```

**Adele convention**: KHÔNG modulate; thay vào đó là **add layer + emotional intensity build**.
**Sam Smith / Lewis Capaldi**: thường modulate +1 (Sam Smith "Stay With Me" final chorus).
**Olivia Rodrigo**: punk-influenced bridge với drums to roar.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Axis (most common) | I V vi IV | C G Am F — drivers license, Sam Smith "Stay With Me" |
| Adele "Someone Like You" | I iii IV I | A C#m D A — gentle lift |
| Adele "Hello" | vi IV I V | Fm Db Ab Eb (in Ab minor) |
| Lewis Capaldi "Someone You Loved" | I V vi IV V | C G Am F G |
| Pop ballad turnaround | I V/vi vi IV | C E7 Am F (secondary dominant) |
| Half-step lift | I → bII (key up) | C → Db (rare but powerful) |

**Trick**: chord change **mỗi 1–2 bar** ở verse, **mỗi 2 bar** ở chorus; secondary dominant V/vi
trước vi tạo "lift" emotional; **suspended chord** (Csus2, Fsus4) intro để mở mood.

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Intro**: piano grand close-mic (Steinway/Bösendorfer) hoặc strings swell; vocal hum hoặc "ohhh"
- **Verse 1**: piano + close-mic vocal + light pad; KHÔNG drums
- **Pre-chorus**: thêm strings legato + light bass; build vocal lên
- **Chorus 1**: piano big + strings full + bass + drums (kick 1+3, snare 2+4 with reverb tail)
- **Verse 2**: piano + bass + drums tiếp; thêm acoustic guitar fingerpicking light
- **Bridge**: strip-down (piano + vocal only) HOẶC big drum drop (Olivia Rodrigo style)
- **Final Chorus**: tom rolls intro, vocal stack 3–4 part (lead + 3rd + octave + ad-lib),
  guitar electric clean enter, big snare gated reverb 0.6–0.8s
- **Outro**: piano tag với vocal "ohhh" or "I love you" final whisper

## 6. Vocal style

- **Tone**: belt-friendly soulful (Adele/Sam Smith); quiet intimate (Birdy); raspy emotional (Lewis Capaldi)
- **Phrasing**: pull behind beat (back-phrasing) cho emotional weight; melisma vừa phải
- **Stack**: lead pure verse → +octave below pre-chorus → +3rd above chorus 1 → +5th + ad-lib chorus 2/final
- **Ad-lib**: gospel-style runs cuối (Adele "I'll find someone like you-oo-oo"); "yeah", "oh yeah"
- **Vibrato**: subtle on long notes (3+ second sustain)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.5–2.0s decay generous, predelay 30–40ms

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: love, heartbreak, regret, longing, forgiveness — direct, không metaphor dày
- **Pronoun**: "I", "you", "we" — second-person address phổ biến
- **Imagery**: rain, streets, photographs, phone, bed, mirror, drinks, midnight, "back when"
- **Hook line**: 6–10 syllables, repeatable, anchor concept ("Someone like you", "Stay with me", "Someone you loved")
- **Verse câu 8–12 syllables**, chorus câu 6–10
- **Rhyme scheme**: AABB hoặc ABAB; multi-syllabic rhyme tô đậm (xem `english-rhyme-techniques.md`)
- **Specific details cuộc sống** (concrete imagery): "your old t-shirt", "the kitchen light", "your favorite song"

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng dùng autotune nặng — pop ballad expect raw vocal expressiveness
- Đừng synth lead modern — phải piano grand thật + strings real (hoặc sample VSL/Spitfire chuẩn)
- Đừng bridge 4 bar quá ngắn — phải 8–16 bar emotional pivot
- Đừng modulate quá lộ liễu — Adele KHÔNG modulate, build bằng layer thay vì key change
- Đừng câu hook dài >12 syllable — không nhớ được
- Đừng cliché lyric "broken heart", "tears falling" — dùng concrete details

## 10. Liên hệ persona prompts

- Theorist: axis I-V-vi-IV; secondary dominant V/vi; suspended chord intro; modulate optional
- Composer: hook 6–10 syllables; peak F5 (nữ alto) hoặc G4 (nam baritone); back-phrasing nhẹ
- Lyricist: direct emotional language; concrete details; multi-syllabic rhyme; xem `english-rhyme-techniques.md`
- Arranger: piano-strings-drums core; bridge có thể strip OR drop; final chorus add layer thay modulate
- Producer: −9 đến −7 LUFS; plate verb 1.5–2s; reference Adele/Sam Smith/Lewis Capaldi (English artists OK ở đây, không phải V-pop)
