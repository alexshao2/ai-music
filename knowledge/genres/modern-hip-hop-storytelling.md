# Modern Hip-Hop Storytelling — Cookbook đầy đủ

Storytelling rap (~2010+) là dòng hip-hop ưu tiên narrative + lyric depth thay vì hook-driven
trap. Kendrick Lamar (good kid, m.A.A.d city), J. Cole (4 Your Eyez Only), Tyler, the Creator
(IGOR), Chance the Rapper (Coloring Book), Vince Staples là blueprint. Khác trap mainstream ở chỗ
beat space hơn, vocal phối linh hoạt (rap + sing), structure dài và conceptual.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 70–95 BPM (rap groove half-time feel); J. Cole 78–88 phổ biến; Kendrick 75–90 |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%) |
| Key | Minor dominant ~70% (Cm, Em, Fm, Gm); major cho conceptual lighter (D, F) |
| Vocal range | Rap C3–E4 spoken pitch range; sing G3–E5 (Tyler falsetto C5) |
| Master loudness | −7 đến −10 LUFS streaming |
| Texture | 808 sub + chopped soul/jazz sample + light keys + hi-hat 1/16 + vocal central + minimal layering |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, sample loop hoặc spoken)
→ Verse 1 (16–32 bar — long verse, narrative phải có không gian)
→ Hook / Chorus (8 bar)  →  Verse 2 (16–32)
→ Hook  →  Bridge / interlude (8, có thể beat switch)
→ Verse 3 (16–24, climax narrative)  →  Hook final  →  Outro (4–8, vocal tag hoặc spoken)
```

**Beat switch**: storytelling rap LOVES beat switch giữa bài (Kendrick "DNA.", J. Cole "1985").
Verse 1 một beat, Verse 3 chuyển sang beat mới với same key/tempo.

**Verse dài**: 16 bar minimum, 24–32 bar phổ biến cho narrative. Đừng compress xuống 8 bar.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

Hip-hop sample-based nên chord = sample loop. Nhưng pattern phổ biến:

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Boom-bap loop | i bVI bVII | Cm Ab Bb (jazz minor sample) |
| Conscious soul | I IV vi | C F Am (gospel chord) |
| Jazz minor 7 | i7 iv7 V7 | Cm7 Fm7 G7 (J. Cole) |
| Tyler IGOR | I bIII V | C Eb G (modal mixture) |
| 4 chord soul | Imaj7 vi9 ii7 V7 | Fmaj7 Dm9 Gm7 C7 (jazz cycle) |
| Drone | i (single chord vamp) | Em pedal with melodic motion |

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Drums**: 808 sub + kick + snare 2+4 (sometimes 3 only, lazy) + hi-hat 1/16 with rolls;
  occasional live drum break (Kendrick "King Kunta")
- **Bass**: 808 sub with glide, OR upright bass (jazz), OR synth bass; pocket-deep, not aggressive
- **Keys**: Rhodes, soul piano sample, Wurlitzer; chopped from old soul records
- **Strings/Horns**: occasionally sampled (Bobby Caldwell, Marvin Gaye type)
- **FX**: vinyl crackle, reverse cymbal, subtle riser
- **Vocal**: rap centered, slightly compressed; doubled in hook only; ad-libs scattered
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0s short on rap; longer 1.5s on sing/hook

## 6. Vocal style

- **Rap delivery**: pocket-tight on-beat, occasional triplet flow, 1/16 note phrasing
- **Storytelling voice**: conversational, change tone for character POV
- **Sing-rap hybrid** (Tyler, Drake, Chance): switch fluidly between rapping and melodic
- **Stack**: rap solo (no double except "punch in" emphasis); hook double + harmony
- **Ad-libs**: scattered "yeah", "uh", "skrt", contextual whispers behind
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0s short; tape delay on hook ad-libs
- **Compression**: heavy 4:1 ratio; vocal sit forward in mix

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: personal narrative, growing up, race, family, mental health, ambition, regret
- **Density**: high — verse 16 bar phải có 100+ words ; multi-syllabic rhyme dense (8–12 per bar)
- **Imagery**: streets, neighborhoods, projects, mama's house, school, jail, church, weed/booze concrete
- **Pronoun**: "I" first-person dominant; "we" community; "you" narrative addressee
- **Rhyme**: complex internal multi-syllabic (xem `english-rhyme-techniques.md`)
- **Hook short** 4–8 words, often question or statement
- **Verse 16 bar = ~120–180 syllables**; rap pace
- **Concrete details**: brand names (Polo, Air Jordan), street names (Compton, Fayetteville),
  family member names, specific years/dates

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng trap mumble — storytelling expects clear articulation
- Đừng verse 8 bar — phải 16–32 bar narrative
- Đừng synth lead aggressive (drill-style) — phải sample-based or live keys
- Đừng auto-tune toàn bộ vocal — chỉ ad-lib hoặc sing portion
- Đừng hook 16 bar — hook phải ngắn 4–8 bar to leave room for verse
- Đừng skip vinyl crackle — texture essential cho conscious rap

## 10. Liên hệ persona prompts

- Theorist: jazz minor 7, soul chord; tempo 70–95; minor key dominant
- Composer: verse 16–32 bar; hook 4–8 bar short; beat switch optional ở verse 3
- Lyricist: dense narrative; multi-syllabic internal rhyme; concrete neighborhood/family details; xem `english-rhyme-techniques.md`
- Arranger: 808 + sample + light keys + hi-hat 1/16; texture sparse; beat switch giữa bài
- Producer: −7 đến −10 LUFS; vinyl crackle; sample-based feel; reference Kendrick/J. Cole/Tyler
