# Country Modern

Country modern là dòng nhạc Mỹ đặc trưng với pedal steel guitar, fiddle, banjo, acoustic
guitar fingerstyle, storytelling lyric Americana. 2010s+ phát triển nhiều nhánh: bro-country
(Luke Bryan, Florida Georgia Line), modern country pop (Luke Combs, Morgan Wallen, Sam Hunt),
indie country (Kacey Musgraves, Tyler Childers, Sturgill Simpson), country pop crossover
(Taylor Swift early, Carrie Underwood).

## Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 80–130 BPM (ballad 80-100, mid-tempo 110-120, upbeat 125-130) |
| Key | Major key dominant (G, D, A, E, C, B♭) — country tradition major; minor (Em, Am, Dm) modern indie |
| Time sig | 4/4 chủ yếu; 3/4 (waltz) cho country waltz traditional |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Chorus – Verse 2 – Chorus – Bridge – Chorus – Outro |
| Duration | 3:00–4:00 (radio standard) |

## Cấu trúc song-form

```
Intro (4-8 bar) — acoustic guitar fingerstyle riff
Verse 1 (16 bar) — vocal storytelling, drum brushed light, bass walking
Chorus (16 bar) — full band, pedal steel, vocal stack 3-part
Verse 2 (16 bar) — different lyric, same harmonic
Chorus (16 bar) — repeat with slight variation
Bridge (8-16 bar) — modulation possible, instrumental break (fiddle/pedal steel solo)
Chorus (16 bar) — final, tom rolls, vocal full
Outro (4-8 bar) — fade with acoustic guitar
```

## Vòng hợp âm

### Country major (most common)

```
I – V – vi – IV (G – D – Em – C) — axis "Country pop"
I – IV – V (G – C – D) — three-chord traditional
I – V/V – V – I (G – A – D – G) — secondary dominant for "twang"
I – IV – I – V – I (G – C – G – D – G) — 12-bar inspired
```

### Country minor (modern indie)

```
i – bVII – IV – i (Em – D – C – Em) — modern country indie
i – V – bVI – iv (Em – B – C – Am) — modern emotional
```

### Capo logic (country signature)

Country guitarists DEMAND capo cho keys không "guitar-friendly" — capo to play in G/D/A/E shapes.
Common: capo 2 (D shape → E), capo 4 (G shape → B), capo 5 (G shape → C).

## Drum / rhythm pattern

**Country shuffle** (mid-tempo):
```
Kick:   X . . . X . . . X . . . X . . . (4-on-floor light)
Snare:  . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . . (beat 2 + 4)
HiHat:  X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X . (8th-note shuffle 60-65%)
```

**Country ballad slow**:
```
Kick:   X . . . . . . . X . . . . . . . (beat 1 + 3 only)
Snare:  . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . . (brushed)
HiHat:  X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X . (8th-note shuffle)
```

**Bro-country / pop crossover** (Sam Hunt, Luke Bryan):
```
Kick:   X . . . X . . . X . . . X . . . (4-on-floor)
Clap:   . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . . (replace snare with clap pop)
HiHat:  . X . X . X . X (off-beat 8th)
```

## Phối khí

### Intro

- Acoustic guitar fingerstyle (Travis pick or rolling 1/8)
- Bass enters bar 5
- Light brush drums

### Verse

- Acoustic guitar fingerstyle (continuous)
- Bass walking quarters
- Brushed drums light kick + brushed snare
- Maybe pedal steel pad (sustained chord)
- Vocal storytelling

### Chorus

- Full band: acoustic guitar strum (rhythm) + electric guitar arpeggio (lead) +
  pedal steel sliding lines + bass + drums full kit
- Vocal stack 3-part (lead + 3rd above + harmony low)
- Plate verb 1.0-1.4s

### Bridge / Instrumental break

- **PEDAL STEEL SOLO** (signature country) — sliding melodic lines, vibrato
- Or **FIDDLE SOLO** (traditional) — bowed fast 1/16
- Or **BANJO SOLO** (bluegrass-influenced) — picking 1/16 forward roll
- Drums lock pattern, chord cycle continues
- Vocal silent

### Final Chorus

- All elements + tom rolls
- Vocal stack 4-part
- Pedal steel countermelody

### Outro

- Acoustic guitar fingerstyle solo or full chord strum
- Fade or final cadence I-V-I

## Vocal style

- **Twang**: subtle Southern accent (vowel shifts: "I" → "ah"; "right" → "raht")
- **Slide / portamento** entry to note
- Vibrato medium (4-6 Hz, ±30 cent)
- Vocal stack 3-part chorus standard
- Vocal range: nữ A3-E5 (alto/mezzo); nam C3-G4 (baritone/tenor)
- KHÔNG autotune heavy — country giữ tự nhiên human (subtle correction OK)

## Lyric idiom

- **Storytelling 1st-person** — specific people, places, time
- **Specific imagery**: dirt road, pickup truck, pickup truck (yes, twice), cold beer, mom's kitchen,
  porch swing, screen door, rain on windshield, gravel driveway, hay bale, tractor, grain silo,
  Tennessee whiskey, denim, boots, neon sign, gas station, jukebox, county fair, bonfire
- **Brand names**: Chevy, Ford, John Deere, Wrangler, Budweiser, Coors Light, Carhartt, Marlboro
  (real brands, not generic)
- **Locations**: small towns, county names, river names, Mississippi/Tennessee/Texas,
  Main Street, Highway 65
- **Themes**: love (lost/found/forever), home/family, drinking, working blue-collar, freedom,
  Sunday church, mama, daddy, summer nights
- Pronoun "I" / "you" / "we" / "ma" / "daddy"
- Rhyme AAB or AABB, slant rhyme acceptable (stressed vowel match, consonant slight differ)
- KHÔNG abstract / academic vocabulary — country = concrete, plain-spoken
- **Bro-country**: trucks + beer + girls + dirt road (formula). Modern indie (Kacey/Tyler):
  more nuanced storytelling.

## DO / DON'T

**DO**:
- Acoustic guitar fingerstyle FOUNDATION
- Pedal steel sliding lines (signature)
- Fiddle hoặc banjo solo bridge break (16-bar)
- Storytelling lyric với concrete imagery (truck, road, beer, mom)
- Brand names real (Chevy, Coors)
- Vocal twang Southern accent subtle
- Vocal stack 3-part chorus
- Major key dominant tradition

**DON'T**:
- KHÔNG synthesizer / drum machine ở verse/chorus (nếu có, chỉ ở pop crossover)
- KHÔNG autotune heavy (country giữ tự nhiên)
- KHÔNG K-pop / J-pop hyper-modern syntheszied chorus
- KHÔNG abstract academic lyric
- KHÔNG lyric thiếu concrete detail
- KHÔNG dembow / 4-on-floor (đó không phải country)
- KHÔNG English code-mix Asian language

## Persona linkage

- **Theorist**: tempo 80-130 BPM (ballad 80-100, mid 110-120, upbeat 125-130); key major
  G/D/A/E/C/B♭ tradition, minor Em/Am modern indie; chord I-V-vi-IV axis hoặc I-IV-V
  three-chord; secondary dominant V/V for "twang"; modulate +1 final chorus possible.
- **Composer**: form Intro/V1/Chorus/V2/Chorus/Bridge instrumental 16 bar/Final Chorus/Outro.
  Vocal range alto C4-D5 (nữ), baritone C3-G4 (nam). Phrase 8-14 syllable verse,
  6-10 syllable chorus.
- **Lyricist**: storytelling 1st-person, specific concrete imagery (truck, road, beer, mama),
  real brand names (Chevy, Coors), Southern locations (Tennessee, Mississippi). Theme love/
  home/drinking/summer. Vocabulary plain-spoken, không abstract.
- **Arranger**: acoustic guitar fingerstyle foundation + pedal steel + fiddle/banjo + electric
  guitar arpeggio + bass walking + brushed drums. Bridge: pedal steel/fiddle/banjo SOLO
  16-bar.
- **Producer**: LUFS −9 to −11; analog warm tape saturation; vocal stack 3-part chorus;
  reference Luke Combs / Morgan Wallen / Kacey Musgraves / Chris Stapleton. KHÔNG Asian
  refs cho country.
