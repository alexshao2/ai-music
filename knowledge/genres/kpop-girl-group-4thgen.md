# K-pop 4th Gen Girl Group — Cookbook đầy đủ

K-pop 4th generation (2022+) là cuộc cách mạng sound: bỏ EDM big-room/trap aggressive 3rd gen,
quay về **UK Garage / 2-step / Plastic Love city-pop / synth-funk / Y2K nostalgia**. Min Hee-jin
(NewJeans creative director) là kiến trúc sư chính. NewJeans "Hype Boy" đặt blueprint UK Garage
revival. aespa "Spicy" Y2K, IVE "I AM" pop confidence, LE SSERAFIM "Antifragile" Latin-influenced.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 100–135 BPM. UK Garage 130 BPM (NewJeans signature); Y2K 110–130; Latin-influenced 100–120 |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%); 2-step shuffle hi-hat tạo feel 1/16 swing |
| Key | Major dominant ~65% (E♭, F, G, A); minor cho moodier (Am, F#m) |
| Vocal range | E4–F#5 (NewJeans members slim range, KHÔNG belt cao G5+ như 3rd gen); breathy delivery |
| Master loudness | −7 đến −9 LUFS (less hyper-compressed hơn 3rd gen) |
| Texture | UK Garage 2-step drums + plastic-love bass + Rhodes/synth pluck + breathy vocal stack 3-part |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form 4th gen)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, instrumental hook hoặc vocal teaser)
→ Verse 1 (8–16, sparse beat with vocal close-mic)
→ Pre-chorus (4–8, build subtle)
→ Chorus 1 (16, hook lặp 2-4×, KHÔNG drop EDM big-room)
→ Verse 2 (8)  →  Pre  →  Chorus 2
→ Bridge (8, beat switch hoặc strip)
→ Final Chorus (16, layer add chứ KHÔNG modulate big)
→ Outro (4–8 fade)
```

**4th gen signature**: KHÔNG có rap break dài như 3rd gen; KHÔNG dance break instrumental 16 bar
truyền thống. Thay vào đó là **micro-hook density** — câu hook ngắn 3-5 từ lặp lại 4-8× per chorus.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| UK Garage 2-step | Imaj7 vi9 ii7 V7 | Cmaj7 Am9 Dm7 G7 (jazz cycle borrowed) |
| Plastic Love loop | iim7 V7 IIImaj7 vi7 | Dm7 G7 Emaj7 Am7 (chromatic mediant — NewJeans signature) |
| Y2K aespa | I IV vi V | C F Am G — happy bounce |
| Latin-influenced | i bVI bVII | Am F G — LE SSERAFIM "Antifragile" |
| Modal funk | I bVII IV | C Bb F (mixolydian color) |
| Static loop | i pedal + sample | Em pedal + soul sample chop |

**Trick**: chord change every 4 bar (rất chậm so với 3rd gen 1 bar/chord chorus); jazz extended
chord (maj7, m9, ø7) borrow từ city-pop / UK Garage; bass line walking 1/8 note giữa chord roots.

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Drums**: 2-step UK Garage signature — kick on 1 + sometimes 2.5; snare on 2 + 4 with light tap;
  hi-hat shuffled 1/16 with swing 50–60%; KHÔNG 4-on-floor; KHÔNG trap hi-hat 1/32 aggressive
- **Bass**: synth bass deep sub (Sub37) + electric bass guitar layer; walking 1/8 note groove
- **Keys**: Rhodes Mark II (verse) + Wurlitzer (chorus) + DX7 epiano (Y2K accent); plucked synth (Juno-60)
- **Guitar**: clean Stratocaster với chorus FX; minimal use, ambient layer only
- **Synth**: warm Juno pad, Plastic-Love-style; occasional vocoder accent
- **Vocal**: close-mic doubled, breathy soft, KHÔNG belt; airy head voice
- **FX**: tape saturation entire master subtle, vinyl crackle low-volume hidden, plate verb 1.0–1.5s
- **Reverb**: gentle plate 1.2s, KHÔNG gated 80s reverb

## 6. Vocal style

- **Tone**: breathy soft close-mic (NewJeans), confident sweet (IVE), edgy (aespa), powerful (LE SSERAFIM)
- **Phrasing**: precise on-beat hoặc slight back-phrasing (jazz-influenced for NewJeans)
- **Stack**: lead + 1 doubled (light) + 3rd above; minimal compared to 3rd gen 5-part stacks
- **Ad-libs**: minimal whisper "uh", "yeah", "ah" — không gospel runs
- **Rap**: minimal hoặc hoàn toàn KHÔNG có (4th gen drift away from rap break)
- **Auto-tune**: subtle, không nặng như 3rd gen

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: teen first-love crushes, Y2K nostalgia, confidence, friendship, **không heavy heartbreak**
- **Pronoun**: 너 (neo), 나 (na), 우리 (uri); informal, conversational
- **English code-mix HIGH**: 50–70% English in chorus + entire bridges English (NewJeans goes
  almost full English chorus)
- **Hook ULTRA-SHORT** 2–5 syllables, lặp 4–8× ("Hype boy", "OMG", "I AM", "Antifragile")
- **Imagery**: pop culture references (Y2K aesthetic), summer, school, music notes; less Korean traditional
- **Verse câu 6–10 syllables Korean** + English insertions; chorus câu 3–8

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng EDM drop big-room — đó là 3rd gen aesthetic
- Đừng trap hi-hat 1/32 aggressive — phải shuffled 2-step
- Đừng vocal belt G5+ — 4th gen vocal range hẹp hơn intentional
- Đừng rap break 16 bar — 4th gen drift away
- Đừng modulate +1 final chorus — 4th gen layer add thay vì key change
- Đừng heavy English code-mix mọi nơi — bridge thường full English nhưng verse phần lớn Korean
- Đừng vocal stack 5-part — 4th gen stack 2-3 part max
