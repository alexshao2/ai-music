# Bachata — Cookbook đầy đủ

Bachata là dòng romantic Latin-Caribbean origin Dominican Republic (1960s+). **Guitar requinto**
(small high-pitched guitar with arpeggio fingerstyle) là backbone signature. Modern urban bachata
(Aventura, Romeo Santos, Prince Royce, 2000s+) thêm R&B vocal + production polish, đem bachata
ra global mainstream. Juan Luis Guerra là patron saint truyền thống bachata literary.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 110–130 BPM (medium uptempo lock); thường 120 BPM |
| Time sig | 4/4 (95%); occasional 4/4 với 3-3-2 syncopation |
| Key | Major dominant ~70% (D, F, G, A, C); minor cho heartbreak (Am, Em, Bm) |
| Vocal range | Spanish romantic: nữ A3–E5; nam B2–G4 with falsetto C5 (Prince Royce, Romeo) |
| Master loudness | −8 đến −10 LUFS |
| Texture | Guitar requinto (lead) + segunda guitar (rhythm) + bass + bongó + güira + maracas + romantic vocal |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, requinto guitar arpeggio signature)
→ Verse 1 (16, vocal + requinto + segunda)
→ Pre-chorus (8, build với percussion increase)
→ Chorus / Coro (16, hook lặp 4×)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Pre-chorus  →  Coro
→ Mambo / Instrumental break (8–16, requinto solo improvised)
→ Bridge / Puente (8, breakdown OR vocal climax)
→ Final Coro (16, layer max + ad-lib peak)
→ Outro (4–8, requinto tag fade)
```

**Bachata signature**: ALWAYS có instrumental requinto solo 8-16 bar (gọi là "mambo") trước final
chorus — convention bắt buộc. Modern bachata (Romeo) sometimes skip if structure too long, but
classic always has it.

## 3. Bachata rhythm — backbone

```
Bongó:    X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X .   (1/8 constant)
Güira:    X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X   (1/16 scrape, "shhh" sound)
Maracas:  . X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X   (off-beat 1/8)
Bass:     X . . X . X . X . . X . X . . X   (3-3-2 syncopation pattern)
Guitar:   Arpeggio fingerstyle 1/16 with chord change on 1+3
```

The **3-3-2 bass syncopation** is bachata's rhythmic DNA. Bass plays 3-3-2 pattern across the
4-beat bar, creating signature lilt that distinguishes bachata from other Latin genres.

## 4. Vòng hợp âm hay

Bachata uses pop chord with Latin twist:

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Bachata classic | I V vi IV | C G Am F |
| Romeo Santos style | I iii IV V | C Em F G |
| Heartbreak minor | i bVI bIII bVII | Am F C G |
| Modal bachata | i bIII bVII iv | Em G D Am |
| Modulation final | I → +1 | C → C# |
| Add9 romantic | Iadd9 V vi IV | Cadd9 G Am F |

**Trick**: chord change every 1-2 bar; **suspended/add9 voicings** trên requinto cho mood romantic;
**secondary dominant V/vi** dồn trước vi tạo lift.

## 5. Phối khí per section

- **Guitar requinto (lead)**: high-pitched 6-string guitar, fingerstyle arpeggio 1/16; signature voice
- **Guitar segunda (rhythm)**: regular acoustic guitar, strums chord pattern 1+3 + syncopated 2.5+4.5
- **Bass**: electric bass guitar, 3-3-2 syncopation pattern
- **Percussion**: bongó (1/8 constant), güira (1/16 scrape), maracas (off-beat 1/8); occasional cajón
- **Drums**: light drum kit (modern urban bachata) OR no kit (classic); brushed snare 2+4
- **Vocal**: lead solo verse → harmonies enter chorus (often 2-3 part Aventura-style stack)
- **Romantic FX**: plate verb 1.5s on vocal, slap delay 1/4 on tail
- **Modern urban bachata**: thêm Rhodes EP layer, light synth pad for atmosphere; classic bachata không có

## 7. Vocal style

- **Tone**: smooth romantic falsetto (Prince Royce, Romeo Santos verse); robust tenor (Romeo Santos chorus); literary baritone (Juan Luis Guerra)
- **Phrasing**: precise on-beat with slight drag; heavy emotional emphasis
- **Falsetto USE**: very common in modern bachata, especially verse breaks
- **Stack**: lead solo verse → +octave above (falsetto layer) → +3rd above chorus → +5th + ad-lib final
- **Ad-libs**: "oh oh", "ay", "mi amor"; whispered "shhh" tag (Romeo Santos signature)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.5s, predelay 30ms; slap delay 1/4 on hook tail

## 8. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: love (predominantly), heartbreak, infidelity (signature Aventura), longing, romance, sensuality
- **Pronoun**: "tú" / "yo" formal romantic; "mi amor" / "mi vida" / "mi cielo" pet names
- **Imagery Spanish**: corazón, alma (soul), beso (kiss), abrazo (hug), lluvia (rain), noche (night),
  lágrimas (tears), promesa (promise), mentira (lie)
- **Hook line** 6–12 syllables Spanish: "Eres mía, sólo mía" (8), "Propuesta indecente" (7)
- **Verse câu 10–14 syllables**, chorus câu 8–12
- **Rhyme**: Spanish assonant -ada / -ido / -ón / -ito; multi-syllabic in chorus
- **Drama / story-telling**: bachata lyric thường có narrative arc (xanh: bài hát Aventura "Obsesión" về
  obsessive crush, "Te Extraño" về missing ex)

## 10. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng skip requinto — guitar requinto IS bachata; without it = không phải bachata
- Đừng skip 3-3-2 bass syncopation — rhythmic DNA
- Đừng dembow rhythm — đó là reggaeton, không phải bachata
- Đừng tempo < 110 hoặc > 130 — strict bachata range
- Đừng vocal English code-mix nặng — bachata stays Spanish 95%+
- Đừng skip mambo/instrumental break before final chorus — convention bắt buộc
- Đừng cliché "mi amor te quiero siempre" — modern bachata tinh tế hơn ("me complicas existencia")
