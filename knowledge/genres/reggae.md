# Reggae — Cookbook đầy đủ

Reggae sinh ra ở Jamaica cuối 1960s từ ska → rocksteady → reggae. Bob Marley (& The Wailers),
Peter Tosh, Jimmy Cliff, Burning Spear đưa reggae lên bản đồ thế giới. Ngày nay có 3 nhánh
chính: **roots reggae** (truyền thống), **dub** (production-focused remix), và **modern roots /
reggae revival** (Chronixx, Protoje, Koffee, Damian Marley). Đặc trưng xuyên suốt: **off-beat
guitar "skank"**, **one-drop drum pattern**, bass guitar chậm + nặng, lyric ý thức xã hội /
Rastafari / love / unity.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 60–90 BPM. Roots 65–80 (Bob Marley); dub 60–75; modern reggae 80–90 |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%) |
| Key | Major dominant ~55% (G, C, D, A, Bb); minor cho rebel/protest (Am, Em, Dm, Gm) |
| Vocal range | Nam C3–G4 (baritone-tenor); nữ A3–E5 |
| Master loudness | −12 đến −8 LUFS (vintage); modern −8 đến −6 LUFS |
| Spring reverb | Có — đặc trưng dub/reggae, đặc biệt trên snare/vocal |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

### Roots reggae (classic)
```
Intro (4–8, guitar skank + bass drop)
→ Verse 1 (16, vocal storytelling)
→ Chorus (8–16, singalong anthem hook)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Chorus
→ Instrumental / Dub break (8–16, bass + drums + effects)
→ Verse 3 (8–16)  →  Chorus (repeat 2–3× fade)
→ Outro (fade with chorus + ad-lib)
```

### Modern reggae (revival)
```
Intro (4–8)
→ Verse 1 (16)  →  Pre-chorus (4–8)
→ Chorus (8–16)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Pre-chorus  →  Chorus
→ Bridge (8, vocal or instrumental)
→ Final Chorus (16, layered)
→ Outro (8, dub-out)
```

**Fade-out** vẫn phổ biến trong reggae, đặc biệt khi chorus lặp.

## 3. One-Drop drum pattern — backbone signature

```
Kick:   . . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (beat 3 ONLY — "one drop")
Snare:  . . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (rim-shot/cross-stick on 3, layered with kick)
Hi-hat: X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X .   (1/8 constant, closed)
```

**One-drop** = kick + snare cùng rơi vào BEAT 3, beat 1 TRỐNG hoàn toàn. Đây là signature
reggae rhythm — nghe là biết reggae.

Biến thể **rockers** (Sly Dunbar):
```
Kick:   X . . . X . . . X . . . X . . .   (mọi beat — 4-on-floor)
Snare:  . . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (vẫn beat 3)
```

## 4. Vòng hợp âm điển hình

| Tên vòng | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Roots major | I–IV–V–I | G–C–D–G. Happy, anthem. |
| Roots minor | i–bVII–bVI–V | Am–G–F–E. Rebel, tense. |
| Reggae turnaround | I–V–vi–IV | G–D–Em–C (Axis). Uplifting. |
| One-chord vamp | I (single chord groove) | G major pedal — groove-driven. |
| Dub minor | i–iv (two-chord loop) | Am–Dm. Hypnotic, repetitive. |
| Bob Marley soul | I–vi–ii–V | G–Em–Am–D. Circle of fifths. |
| Modal mixolydian | I–bVII–IV | G–F–C. Laidback, sunshine feel. |
| Ska upstroke | I–IV | G–C (fast tempo ska). |

**Rhythm guitar "skank"**: chỉ chơi OFF-BEAT (and of 1, and of 2, and of 3, and of 4). Staccato
mute ngay sau khi strum. Đây là element signature nhất — PHẢI có.

## 5. Phối khí per section

| Section | Lớp âm thanh |
|--|--|
| Intro | Guitar skank off-beat + bass drop note + one-drop drums count-in. Hoặc dub echo intro. |
| Verse | Guitar skank (clean Strat/Tele, neck pickup), bass guitar (deep, root-5th pattern, legato), one-drop drums, organ bubble (Hammond, 1/8 off-beat staccato), occasional horn stab. |
| Chorus | Add backing vocals (3-part harmony, "ooh"/"yeah"), horn section (trumpet + sax + trombone melody), percussion (bongo, shaker, cowbell). |
| Dub break | Bass + drums only. Guitar stripped. Delay/echo effects thrown on snare + vocal snippets. Spring reverb. |
| Bridge | Horn solo hoặc guitar solo (wah-wah). Hoặc vocal a cappella + percussion only. |
| Final Chorus | Full arrangement + vocal ad-lib + horn counter-melody + percussion double. |
| Outro | Dub-out: effects-heavy, delay throws, spring reverb swells, instruments fade one by one. |

**Organ bubble**: Hammond organ chơi staccato off-beat cùng guitar skank — tạo "bubble" sound. Đây là layer thứ 2 quan trọng nhất sau guitar skank.

**Bass**: rất nặng, deep, melodic. Thường dùng Fender P-Bass hoặc upright bass. Bass line đi melodic (root-3rd-5th-octave), KHÔNG chỉ root.

## 7. Vocal style

- **Chanting / toasting**: vocal rhythmic nửa nói nửa hát, đặc biệt ở verse (roots tradition)
- **Melodic singing**: chorus thường melodic hơn verse, singalong quality
- **Patois / Jamaican Creole accent**: authentic reggae dùng Jamaican dialect
- **Call-and-response**: lead + backing vocal group
- **Falsetto use**: thỉnh thoảng (Jacob Miller, newer artists)
- **Harmony**: 3-part backing (3rd + 5th above), gospel-influenced
- **Reverb**: spring reverb (đặc trưng dub), plate reverb 1.5s
- **Delay**: dub delay throws — 1/4 note + 1/2 note feedback delay effect

## 8. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: unity, peace, love, Jah/Rastafari, social justice, resistance, marijuana, nature, Africa/repatriation, poverty
- **Tone**: positive/uplifting (roots), rebellious (protest), spiritual (Rasta), romantic
- **Language**: Jamaican Patois mixed với standard English
- **Imagery**: sunshine, ocean, Zion, Babylon (oppressive system), mountains, rivers, one love
- **Hook**: singalong anthem — "No Woman No Cry", "One Love", "Three Little Birds"
- **Repetition**: hook lặp nhiều lần, mantra-like
- **Rasta vocabulary**: "Jah", "Babylon", "I and I" (thay "we"), "Zion", "irie", "ital"

## 9. Suno style tag template

Roots reggae:
```
roots reggae, one-drop rhythm, 78 bpm, g major, off-beat guitar skank,
deep bass guitar, organ bubble, horn section, three-part backing harmony,
warm male vocal, jamaican style, positive vibes, analog warmth
```

Dub:
```
dub reggae, 70 bpm, a minor, deep sub bass, one-drop drums, spring reverb,
echo delay throws, sparse arrangement, psychedelic, analog tape effects
```

Modern reggae revival:
```
modern reggae, reggae revival, 86 bpm, b flat major, clean guitar skank,
deep bass, one-drop drums, brass section, crisp production, conscious lyrics
```

## 10. DON'T

- KHÔNG bỏ off-beat guitar skank — đây là DNA reggae.
- KHÔNG đặt kick vào beat 1 trong one-drop — beat 1 PHẢI trống.
- KHÔNG dùng 808/programmed drums heavy — reggae cần live drum feel organic.
- KHÔNG skip bass prominence — bass là "king" trong reggae mix, louder than guitar.
- KHÔNG để rhythm straight — reggae cần laid-back feel, slightly behind the beat.
