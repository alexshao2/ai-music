# Jazz Standard / American Songbook

Jazz standard = repertoire bài hát phổ biến phát triển 1920-1960 từ Tin Pan Alley + Broadway
(George Gershwin, Cole Porter, Irving Berlin, Rodgers & Hammerstein, Jerome Kern). Form đặc
trưng AABA 32-bar, swing rhythm, ii-V-I cadence, walking bass, comping piano. Đại diện cover:
Ella Fitzgerald, Frank Sinatra, Sarah Vaughan, Billie Holiday, Nat King Cole, Diana Krall,
Norah Jones, Michael Bublé, Tony Bennett.

## Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | Ballad 60-80 BPM, Medium 100-130 BPM, Swing/Up 130-220 BPM |
| Key | Bất kỳ — jazz transposes flexibly. Common: B♭, E♭, A♭, F (horn-friendly); C, G (vocal-friendly) |
| Time sig | 4/4 standard; 3/4 jazz waltz (Bill Evans "Waltz for Debby"); 6/8 occasional |
| Form | **AABA 32-bar** (8+8+8+8) classic standard form; ABAC, ABAB also exist |
| Duration | Single chorus 1:00-2:30; full performance 3-7 minutes (with improv solos) |

## Cấu trúc song-form

### AABA 32-bar (most common)

```
A1 (8 bar) — main melody, ii-V-I in home key
A2 (8 bar) — repeat A1 with possible variation/tag
B  (8 bar) — bridge, contrasting key (often related minor or IV), ii-V's modulating
A3 (8 bar) — return to A melody, final cadence
```

### ABAC (variation)

```
A (8) — main theme
B (8) — second theme contrast
A (8) — return main
C (8) — coda, different ending
```

### Jazz performance form

```
Head (1 chorus) — vocalist sings melody once
Solos (2-4 choruses) — instrumentalists improvise on chord changes
Trade 4s/8s — solo trade with drums
Head out (1 chorus) — vocalist returns melody
Outro tag — final cadence, fade or button
```

## Vòng hợp âm

### Jazz fundamental: ii-V-I

```
Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 (ii-V-I in C major)
Dm7♭5 – G7♭9 – Cm7 (ii-V-i in C minor)
```

### Common standard chord cycles

```
"Autumn Leaves" Em form: Am7 – D7 – Gmaj7 – Cmaj7 – F#m7♭5 – B7♭9 – Em – Em (8 bar A)
"All the Things You Are" Fm form: Fm7 – B♭m7 – E♭7 – A♭maj7 – Dm7♭5 – G7 – Cmaj7 – Cmaj7
"Rhythm Changes" (B♭): B♭ – G7 – Cm7 – F7 – Dm7 – G7 – Cm7 – F7 (Gershwin "I Got Rhythm")
"Blue Bossa" Cm: Cm7 – Cm7 – Fm7 – Fm7 – Dm7♭5 – G7♭9 – Cm7 – Cm7
"Misty" E♭: E♭maj7 – B♭m7 – E♭7 – A♭maj7 – A♭m7 – D♭7 – E♭maj7 – E♭maj7
"Body and Soul" D♭: D♭maj7 – Bm7 – E7 – A♭m7 – D♭7 – Gmaj7 – Em7 – A7
```

### Tritone substitution

Replace V7 with bII7 (same tritone interval inside chord):
```
Standard: Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7
Tritone:  Dm7 – D♭7 – Cmaj7 (chromatic descent root)
```

## Drum / rhythm pattern

**Swing 8th** (signature jazz feel):
```
Beat:   1   2   3   4
8th:    1 . . . 2 . . .  (eighth notes triplet feel — first 2/3 of triplet)
Ride:   X   X   X   X    (quarter ride dominant)
Cymbal: X . X X . X X . X X . X X . X X (ride pattern with skip-skip-skip)
Snare:  . X . . . X . . (off-beat 2 + 4 ghost note "comp")
Kick:   X . . . X . . . (quarter on 1 + 3, soft, "feathered")
```

**Walking bass** (signature):
```
Bass: 1 chord tone per beat, mostly quarter notes, walking through scale tones
       moving up/down by step or 3rd toward next chord root
```

**Brushed drums** (ballad):
```
Snare brush: continuous sweep with "shh-shh-shh" 8th note
Bass drum: very soft, "feathered"
Ride: quarter notes light
```

## Phối khí

### Trio / Quartet (small group)

- Piano (comping chord, brief solos)
- Upright bass (walking)
- Drums (ride + brush)
- Vocalist (lead) or Saxophone (Tenor/Alto) lead

### Quintet (with horns)

- Trumpet + Saxophone (heads in unison or harmony)
- Piano + Bass + Drums (rhythm section)

### Big Band

- 5 saxes (2 alto, 2 tenor, 1 baritone)
- 4 trumpets
- 4 trombones
- Rhythm section (piano + bass + guitar + drums)

### Jazz vocal trio (Norah Jones, Diana Krall style)

- Piano + Bass + Drums + Vocal lead
- Often vocalist plays piano herself

## Vocal style

- **Phrasing behind the beat** — vocalist stays slightly late, "lazy swing" feel
- **Vibrato moderate** — natural, not heavy
- **Scat singing** — improvise melodic syllables (doo-bee-doo-bah-dah-dap) on solos
- **Microtonal slides** — bend pitch slightly between notes
- **Dynamic control** — soft → loud subtle within phrase
- Vocal range: nữ A3-E5 (alto/mezzo); nam C3-G4 (baritone/tenor)
- Diana Krall / Norah Jones style: intimate close-mic, breathy, conversational
- Frank Sinatra style: belting power, swagger, behind-beat phrasing

## Lyric idiom (American Songbook tradition)

- **Romantic narrative** — courtship, longing, heartbreak, swooning
- **Sophisticated vocabulary** — "amour", "heaven", "starlight", "gardenias", "rendezvous"
- **AABB rhyme scheme** classic 32-bar form
- **Internal rhyme** Cole Porter signature: "I get no kick from champagne / mere alcohol doesn't thrill me at all"
- **Imagery palette**: moon, stars, rain, lights, candlelight, garden, midnight, violins, train,
  Paris, Manhattan, beach, sea, sky
- **Topics**: love (always love), nostalgia, daydream, longing, journey, season change
- **Pronoun**: "I" / "you" / "we" — first person classic
- AVOID modern slang / texting language

## DO / DON'T

**DO**:
- AABA 32-bar form classic
- ii-V-I cadence DOMINANT
- Swing 8th rhythm signature
- Walking upright bass
- Comping piano (chord on off-beat)
- Brushed drums (ballad) hoặc ride cymbal lead (medium)
- Chord extensions (maj7, m9, 13, ♭9)
- Tritone substitution OK
- Phrasing behind the beat vocal
- Romantic narrative lyric với sophisticated vocabulary
- Internal rhyme

**DON'T**:
- KHÔNG 4-on-floor kick (jazz NOT EDM/dance)
- KHÔNG synthesizer modern (jazz acoustic foundation)
- KHÔNG autotune (jazz natural human voice)
- KHÔNG dembow rhythm
- KHÔNG K-pop / J-pop hyper-modern
- KHÔNG modern slang lyric ("OMG", "lit", "bestie")
- KHÔNG hook lặp 8× pop K-pop style — jazz hook lặp 1-2× per chorus only
- KHÔNG trap hi-hat 1/32

## Persona linkage

- **Theorist**: tempo 60-220 BPM (ballad 60-80, medium 100-130, swing 130+); key flexible,
  common B♭/E♭/A♭/F horn-friendly, C/G vocal-friendly. Form AABA 32-bar. Chord ii-V-I
  cadence, extensions maj7/m9/13/♭9, tritone substitution. Walking bass.
- **Composer**: AABA 32-bar form. Phrase 8-14 syllable per A section, 6-10 syllable B bridge.
  Vocal range alto A3-E5 (nữ), baritone C3-G4 (nam). Hook melodic memorable singable.
- **Lyricist**: romantic narrative classic American Songbook tradition. Sophisticated vocabulary
  (amour, gardenias, rendezvous). Internal rhyme. AABB scheme. Imagery moon/stars/rain/Paris.
  Pronoun I/you/we. KHÔNG modern slang.
- **Arranger**: trio (piano + bass + drums) hoặc quartet (+ vocal/saxophone). Walking upright
  bass. Comping piano off-beat chord. Brushed drums (ballad) hoặc ride cymbal lead (swing).
  Solo break 1-2 chorus instrumental.
- **Producer**: LUFS −12 to −14 (jazz dynamic preserved, NO over-limiting); plate verb 1.4-1.6s;
  vocal close-mic intimate (Diana Krall / Norah Jones); analog tape saturation;
  reference Ella Fitzgerald / Frank Sinatra / Norah Jones / Diana Krall / Michael Bublé.
