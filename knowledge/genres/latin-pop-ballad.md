# Latin Pop Ballad — Cookbook đầy đủ

Latin Pop Ballad (1990s+) là dòng emotional Spanish-language ballad với piano-led production +
Latin guitar (Spanish nylon guitar) influences. Khác reggaeton ở chỗ slow tempo, no dembow rhythm,
focus vào vocal expression Spanish lyrical. Shakira "Tú" / Maná "Mariposa Traicionera" /
Camilo "Vida de Rico" định nghĩa dòng. Modern subgenre "Sad Spanish" (Camilo, Yatra, Christian
Nodal) ảnh hưởng Mexican regional + bachata light.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 60–100 BPM. Slow 60–80 (Maná emotional); mid 80–100 (Camilo modern) |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~85%); 3/4 hoặc 6/8 cho mariachi/bolero feel (Christian Nodal) |
| Key | Major 50%, minor 50%; minor cho heartbreak (Am, Em, Dm); major cho love (D, F, G) |
| Vocal range | Nữ A3–E5 (Shakira G3–D5 alto); nam A2–G4 (Camilo, Yatra) |
| Master loudness | −8 đến −10 LUFS (loud nhưng dynamic preserve hơn reggaeton) |
| Texture | Piano grand + Spanish nylon guitar + light strings + acoustic bass + brushed drums |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, piano arpeggio hoặc Spanish guitar arpeggio fingerstyle)
→ Verse 1 (16, vocal close-mic + piano/guitar)
→ Pre-chorus (8, build with strings)  →  Chorus (16)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Pre-chorus  →  Chorus
→ Bridge (8–16, strip-down hoặc emotional belt)
→ Final Chorus (16, modulate +1 thường xảy ra Latin ballad)
→ Outro (4–8 fade hoặc final cadence)
```

**Latin ballad signature**: thường modulate +1 trước final chorus (signature từ Mexican ranchera
+ bolero tradition); Spanish guitar fingerstyle xuyên suốt; vocal expression cao.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

Latin pop ballad mượn từ flamenco / bolero / pop ballad:

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Latin pop axis | I V vi IV | C G Am F |
| Bolero classic | I V/iv iv I | C G7 Fm C (modal interchange minor iv) |
| Spanish minor | i bVII bVI V | Am G F E (Phrygian dominant signature flamenco) |
| Modulation final | I → +1 via bIIdom7 | C → C# via G#7 pivot |
| Ranchera 6/8 | I IV V I (in 6/8) | C F G C |
| Add9 ballad | Iadd9 vadd9 vi IV | Cadd9 Gadd9 Am F |

**Trick**: Phrygian dominant (i bVII bVI V) gives flamenco color even in non-flamenco Latin pop;
chord change every 2 bar verse, every 1 bar chorus dồn; **suspended chord** intro.

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Intro**: Spanish nylon guitar fingerstyle + piano arpeggio; vocal "ohhh" optional; KHÔNG drums
- **Verse 1**: piano + Spanish guitar + close-mic vocal + bass enter bar 9
- **Pre-chorus**: thêm strings legato + light brushed drums (kick 1+3, brushed snare 2+4)
- **Chorus 1**: piano big + Spanish guitar continued + strings full + bass + drums (snare reverb 0.6s)
- **Verse 2**: same as verse 1, slight intensity build
- **Bridge**: strip-down (just piano + vocal) HOẶC big drum drop with belt + Latin percussion enter (cajón, shaker)
- **Final Chorus**: modulate +1 BẮT BUỘC Latin tradition; tom rolls intro, vocal stack 3-part
- **Outro**: Spanish guitar tag + vocal "te amo" final

## 6. Vocal style

- **Tone**: full-throated Spanish belt (Shakira, Maná); soft croon (Camilo, Yatra); ranchera vibrato (Nodal)
- **Phrasing**: occasional back-phrasing (jazz/bolero influence); melisma vừa phải
- **Stack**: lead solo verse → +1 doubled pre → +3rd above chorus → +5th + ad-lib chorus 2/final
- **Ad-libs**: emotional "ay", "oh", "te quiero" tail; gospel runs occasional
- **Vibrato**: prominent on long notes (3+ second sustain) — Latin tradition
- **Reverb**: plate 1.4–1.8s decay generous

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: love (te quiero / amor profundo), heartbreak (corazón roto), longing (te extraño), nostalgia
- **Pronoun**: "tú" / "yo" / "nosotros"; informal direct
- **Imagery Spanish**: corazón (heart), luna (moon), estrellas (stars), mariposa (butterfly), beso (kiss),
  lluvia (rain), café, calle (street), abrazo (hug), recuerdo (memory)
- **Hook line** 6–10 syllables Spanish: "te quiero más que nunca" (8), "amor de mi vida" (6)
- **Verse câu 10–14 syllables**, chorus câu 6–10
- **Rhyme**: Spanish assonant common; -or / -ar / -ón / -ito endings; multi-syllabic (xem `spanish-prosody-rhyme.md`)
- **Concrete details**: tên thành phố Mexico/Cuba/Madrid; food (tequila, café, churros); time of day (atardecer, noche)

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng dembow rhythm — đó là reggaeton, không phải ballad
- Đừng skip Spanish nylon guitar — fingerstyle is signature
- Đừng vocal flat — Latin tradition expect vibrato + emotional swells
- Đừng modulate +2 — Latin ballad expect +1 semitone
- Đừng English code-mix nặng — Latin ballad mostly stays Spanish 95%+
- Đừng cliché "mi amor te quiero" thẳng — modern Latin tinh tế hơn ("me complicas la vida")
- Đừng bridge 4 bar — phải 8–16 emotional pivot
