# Modern K-pop — Cookbook đầy đủ

K-pop 3rd generation (2013+) là "song collage" idol-pop: ghép 3-4 motif khác hẳn nhau trong 1 bài
(verse → pre → chorus → rap break → dance break → bridge → final chorus). Khác mọi dòng pop khác
ở chỗ **không một genre nhất quán toàn bài** — verse R&B, chorus EDM, rap break trap, bridge
ballad. Producer là kiến trúc sư chính (Teddy / Pdogg / Boombastic / Jeff Bhasker).

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 80–140 BPM. Mid-tempo 90–110 (BTS "Dynamite"); upbeat 120–140 (BLACKPINK "Kill This Love") |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%) |
| Key | Major 50%, minor 50%; **modulation +1 hoặc +2 trước final chorus** thường xảy ra |
| Vocal range | Nữ E4–G#5 (Rosé belt G5+); nam C3–G4 với falsetto C5 (V/Jungkook) |
| Master loudness | −5 đến −7 LUFS (rất loud, hyper-compressed) |
| Texture | Synth lead + 808 sub + trap hi-hat + distorted bass + dance break loop + vocal stack 5+ part |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form K-pop signature)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, instrumental hook hoặc vocal teaser)
→ Verse 1 (16, R&B style hoặc rap)  →  Pre-chorus (8, build dồn)
→ Chorus 1 (16, EDM/dance/trap, hook lặp 4×)
→ Verse 2 (8, often RAP only)  →  Pre  →  Chorus 2
→ Rap break (8–16, completely different style — trap/dembow)
→ Bridge (8, ballad strip-down hoặc beat switch)
→ Dance break (8, instrumental, choreography signature)
→ Final Chorus (16, modulate +1 hoặc layer max, ad-lib peak)
→ Outro (4–8)
```

**Quy ước K-pop**: phải có ÍT NHẤT 1 trong: rap break, dance break, beat switch — KHÔNG phải
straight pop ballad.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

K-pop borrow từ R&B (i7 iv7 V7), EDM (i bVI III bVII), J-pop Royal Road (IV V iii vi):

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Trap-pop loop | i bVI bIII bVII | Am F C G — BLACKPINK foundation |
| K-R&B verse | i7 iv7 III V7 | Am7 Dm7 C E7 — BTS Spring Day base |
| Future bass | I V vi IV (with extended 9/13) | Cmaj9 G13 Am9 Fmaj9 |
| Hip-hop loop | i (single chord vamp + sample) | Em pedal, sample melodic |
| Modulation final | +1 semitone via bIIdom7 → I | Db7 → C major |
| Dance break tonic | i (single chord, beat-driven) | Loop on tonic, no chord change |

**Trick**: chord change nhanh ở chorus (1 bar/chord), chậm ở verse (2 bar/chord), 0 chord change
ở dance break (single tonic vamp).

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Intro**: instrumental hook teaser (synth lead, vocal "ah-ah", 4-8 bar) — must be memorable
- **Verse 1 (R&B style)**: 808 sub + sparse drums (kick 1, snare 3) + Rhodes/synth pluck + close-mic vocal
- **Pre-chorus**: dồn — kick 1+3, hi-hat 1/16, riser FX synthwave, snare flam at bar 8
- **Chorus 1 (EDM/dance)**: full kit + 808 + 4-on-floor (dance) hoặc trap hi-hat 1/32 + synth lead aggressive + vocal stack 4-part with hyper-stereo
- **Verse 2 (RAP)**: completely different beat — trap drums 1/32 hi-hat, distorted bass, autotuned rap delivery
- **Rap break (8 bar)**: brand new beat (dembow, drill, or dnb); change of energy
- **Bridge**: strip xuống piano + vocal hoặc beat switch sang half-time emotional
- **Dance break (8 bar)**: instrumental, signature loop hook, no vocal — for choreography
- **Final Chorus**: modulate +1 hoặc add layer (gospel choir, doubled instruments, harder snare with gated reverb)
- **Outro**: hook tag fade hoặc final cadence with vocal "ah-ah"

## 6. Vocal style

- **Member rotation**: K-pop song = 7+ members chia line; mỗi member có signature style (lead vocal, rapper, dancer, vocalist sub)
- **Vocal stack**: lead (1 member) + 3rd above (sub vocalist) + 5th (ad-lib) + harmony group (3-4 members background)
- **Belt vocal**: nữ E5–G5 power belt (Rosé, Jisoo, Wendy); nam C5+ falsetto (V, Jungkook, Baekhyun)
- **Rap delivery**: tight pocket, autotuned subtle, English code-mix
- **Ad-libs**: scattered "yeah", "uh", "let's go", "BLACKPINK in your area" tag
- **Vocal stylization**: heavy compression, doubled in chorus, FX (chorus, phaser) on bridge

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: love, confidence, empowerment, dreams, friendship, hardship overcome
- **Pronoun Korean**: 너 (neo, "you" informal), 나 (na, "I" informal), 우리 (uri, "we")
- **English code-mix**: 30–50% English ở chorus + rap break — signature K-pop bilingual
  ("you're so fine, I'm in love", "yeah uh", "let's get it")
- **Hook short** 3–8 syllables, repeat 4× per chorus ("DDU-DU DDU-DU", "Kill This Love", "Boom Boom Boom")
- **Verse câu 6–10 syllables Korean**, chorus câu 4–8 with English
- **Rhyme scheme**: AABB Korean tail rhyme + English chorus

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng quên rap break / dance break — K-pop expect ÍT NHẤT 1 segment "khác chất"
- Đừng vocal solo toàn bài — phải có 5+ member rotation feel (vocal stack escalation)
- Đừng lyric 100% Korean — phải có English code-mix ở chorus + rap break
- Đừng master quiet — K-pop là loudness war winner (−5 to −7 LUFS)
- Đừng bridge straight pop — phải có beat switch hoặc strip-down dramatic
- Đừng cliché "you're my world" thẳng — Korean idiom tinh tế hơn
