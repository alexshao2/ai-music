# Trap — Cookbook đầy đủ

Trap sinh ra từ Southern hip-hop Atlanta đầu 2000s (T.I., Jeezy, Gucci Mane), phát triển qua
**Lex Luger era** (2010–2013), rồi thống trị mainstream qua **Future, Migos, Young Thug, Travis
Scott, 21 Savage** với các producer Metro Boomin, Southside, London on da Track, Murda Beatz.
Đặc trưng: **808 bass cực nặng**, **hi-hat roll 1/32**, **dark minor key**, **triplet flow**,
**auto-tune vocal**. Trap đã trở thành backbone production cho pop, R&B, Latin, K-pop hiện đại.

Biến thể: **drill** (UK/Chicago — slower, darker), **rage** (Playboi Carti — chaotic synth),
**melodic trap** (Juice WRLD, Lil Uzi Vert — singing over trap beats).

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 130–170 BPM (half-time feel = 65–85 BPM groove); sweet-spot 140–150 BPM |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~99%) |
| Key | Minor dominant ~85% (Cm, Em, Fm, Gm, Am, Bbm); major rất hiếm |
| Vocal range | Rap C3–E4; melodic trap A3–E5 (auto-tune, Young Thug) |
| Master loudness | −5 đến −7 LUFS (VERY loud, aggressive) |
| 808 sub | Core element — pitched, gliding, distorted; 30–60 Hz fundamental |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, 808 drop hoặc vocal tag)
→ Chorus / Hook (8, HOOK FIRST — trap thường mở bằng hook)
→ Verse 1 (16, rap/melodic rap)
→ Chorus (8)
→ Verse 2 (16)
→ Chorus (8)
→ Bridge (8, beat switch hoặc strip-down) — optional
→ Outro (4–8, 808 fade hoặc vocal tag)
```

**Hook-first structure**: trap hiện đại thường BẮT ĐẦU bằng hook/chorus trước verse 1.
Tối ưu cho streaming (grab attention trong 10 giây đầu).

**Beat switch**: đổi beat hoàn toàn giữa bài (Travis Scott "SICKO MODE").

## 3. 808 pattern & drum programming

### Core trap drum pattern
```
808:     X . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (half-note, pitched sustain with glide)
Kick:    X . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (layered with 808 transient)
Snare:   . . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (beat 3 — half-time feel)
Hi-hat:  X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X.X  (1/16 constant)
```

### Hi-hat rolls (signature trap element)
```
Hi-hat: X.xxXX.xX.xxXX.x   (x = 1/32 note roll, XX = open hat)
```
Hi-hat rolls 1/32 + occasional 1/64 triplet = signature trap sound. Velocity automation tạo "bounce".

### Variation: drill pattern (UK drill)
```
808:     X . . . X . . . . . X . . . X .   (sliding, more movement)
Snare:   . . . . . . . . X . . . . . . .   (beat 3)
Hi-hat:  X.X.xxX.X.X.xxX.X.X.xxX.X.X.xxX  (1/32 rolls frequent)
```

## 4. Vòng hợp âm điển hình

Trap thường MINIMAL harmonic — 1–2 chord loop hoặc single note melody.

| Tên vòng | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Minor 2-chord | i–bVI | Cm–Ab. Simplest trap loop. |
| Minor 4 | i–bVI–bVII–v | Cm–Ab–Bb–Gm. Standard trap. |
| Dark modal | i (single chord vamp) | Cm pedal + melody on top. |
| Phrygian dark | i–bII | Em–F. Very dark, ominous. |
| Metro Boomin | i–bVII–bVI–V | Am–G–F–E. Dramatic minor. |
| Melodic trap | i–bIII–bVII–iv | Am–C–G–Dm. More harmonic movement. |
| Travis Scott epic | i–bVI–bIII–bVII | Fm–Db–Ab–Eb. Anthemic stadium. |

**Melody**: trap melody thường dùng **minor pentatonic** hoặc **phrygian scale** — dark, ominous.
Synth lead melody ngắn, lặp (2–4 bar loop).

## 5. Phối khí per section

| Section | Lớp âm thanh |
|--|--|
| Intro | 808 sub drop + dark pad + reverse cymbal. Producer tag ("Metro Boomin want some more"). |
| Hook | Full 808 (distorted, gliding), kick layer, snare/clap on 3, hi-hat 1/16 + rolls, dark synth melody (bell, pluck, or pad), vocal auto-tuned + ad-libs. |
| Verse | Same beat, slightly stripped — remove 1 layer (synth hoặc percussion). Vocal rap-flow dominant. |
| Bridge | Beat switch: new 808 pattern, different key, hoặc strip to vocal + 808 only. |
| Outro | 808 sustain tail + vocal tag repeat + reverb tail. |

**808 bass**: the KING of trap production. Pitched (follows chord root), long sustain (1–2 bar),
distortion/saturation for presence, glide between notes (portamento). Side-chain KHÔNG quá hard
— 808 phải nghe đầy, sustained.

**Synth**: dark pad (analog-style), bell/pluck melody (OmniSphere, ElectraX), flute melody
(Metro Boomin signature), piano dark (minor key), string stab.

## 7. Vocal style

- **Auto-tune HEAVY** — T-Pain/Future legacy; pitch correction cranked to "robotic" feel
- **Triplet flow** (Migos "Versace"): 3 syllable per beat, rapid-fire, signature trap cadence
- **Mumble rap**: pronunciation loose, vibe > enunciation (Young Thug, Future)
- **Melodic rap**: singing melodies over trap beats (Juice WRLD, Lil Uzi Vert)
- **Ad-libs**: "skrt", "brr", "yeah", "what", "21", producer tag — constant throughout
- **Double/triple voice**: lead + pitched-up double + pitched-down double
- **Reverb**: hall 1.5–2.5s (spacey, Travis Scott); plate 1.0s (tight, 21 Savage)
- **Delay**: 1/4 note throwback on ad-libs
- **Compression**: heavy 6:1+ ratio; vocal sits FORWARD in aggressive mix

## 8. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: money, flexing/status, drugs (lean/codeine), street life, violence, women, cars, jewelry, loyalty, paranoia
- **Tone**: braggadocious, aggressive, nonchalant, dark; melodic trap = emotional/vulnerable
- **Imagery**: Lamborghini, Patek Philippe, Percocet, choppa, trap house, ice (diamonds), bando
- **Hook**: short 2–4 word phrase, repeated 4–8× ("Bad and Boujee", "Mask Off", "Drip Too Hard")
- **Verse density**: medium — 12–16 syllable/bar, rhyme less complex than boom-bap
- **Slang**: "cap" (lie), "bussin'", "drip", "slime", "no cap", "on God"
- **Brand names**: Gucci, Prada, Rolls Royce, Glock, AP (Audemars Piguet)

## 10. DON'T

- KHÔNG dùng live acoustic drums — trap = programmed 808-based ONLY.
- KHÔNG skip 808 sub bass — nếu không có 808 thì không phải trap.
- KHÔNG để hi-hat straight 1/8 đều — cần rolls 1/32 + velocity variation.
- KHÔNG harmony quá phức tạp — trap minimal, 1–2 chord loop đủ.
- KHÔNG vocal clean/natural — trap cần auto-tune (ít hoặc nhiều).
- KHÔNG nhầm trap tempo: 140 BPM nhưng FEEL half-time (70 BPM groove).
