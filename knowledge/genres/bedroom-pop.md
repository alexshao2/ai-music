# Bedroom Pop / Indie Pop — Cookbook đầy đủ

Bedroom pop (~2017–2025) là DIY low-fi indie pop sản xuất tại nhà với laptop + chitarry + chord
basic. Sound mềm, lo-fi, vocal close-mic không belt, lyric introvert teen-coming-of-age. Khác
indie folk (Vũ./Sufjan) ở chỗ có drum machine + synth, ít acoustic-only.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 80–120 BPM. Slow 80–95 (Clairo "Pretty Girl"); mid 100–115 |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~95%); 6/8 hiếm |
| Key | Major dominant ~70% (C, G, D, F); minor cho moodier (Em, Am) |
| Vocal range | Nữ G3–D5 (Clairo, beabadoobee — không belt cao); nam C3–F4 (Boy Pablo, Rex Orange County) |
| Master loudness | −12 đến −10 LUFS (intentionally NOT loud) |
| Texture | Drum machine LM-1/808 + clean Strat hoặc Jazzmaster + synth pad + soft bass + close-mic vocal |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, guitar riff hoặc synth chord)
→ Verse 1 (16)  →  Chorus (16, có thể KHÔNG có pre-chorus)
→ Verse 2 (16)  →  Chorus
→ Bridge (8, có thể chỉ là instrumental break)
→ Chorus (final)  →  Outro (4–8, tail vocal)
```

**Bedroom pop convention**: form đơn giản hơn pop ballad/J-pop. Không pre-chorus tinh vi, không
modulate. Sometimes 2 verse-chorus rồi outro. Điểm mạnh là **mood + texture**, không phải structure.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Indie axis | I V vi IV (relaxed feel) | C G Am F |
| Lo-fi loop | Imaj7 IVmaj7 vi IV | Cmaj7 Fmaj7 Am F |
| Mac DeMarco wonk | I IV ii V (jazz-inflected) | C F Dm G |
| Clairo "Bags" | vi IV I V (fluid mix) | Am F C G |
| Sus chord | Csus2 Asus2 Fsus2 Gsus2 | Open voicings, no clear major/minor |
| Modal mixture | I bVII bVI bVII | C Bb Ab Bb (Phantogram-like) |

**Trick**: chord change **mỗi 2 bar slow**, cho phép groove settle; **maj7/sus2** voicing dày
nhưng không sharp; **chorus pedal effect** trên electric guitar = signature.

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Drums**: drum machine (LinnDrum LM-1, Roland TR-808) hoặc programmed kit; **không live kit**
  — điều khiến sound bedroom-pop đặc trưng. Hi-hat 1/8, snare 2+4, kick 1+3, ghost note 16th rare
- **Bass**: synth bass (Sub37, MS-20) HOẶC electric jazz bass; soft, sub-heavy, laid-back
- **Guitar**: Stratocaster hoặc Jazzmaster CLEAN với chorus + reverb generous; arpeggio hoặc strummed light
- **Synth**: pad warm (Juno-60, Prophet-5); plucked synth (DX7 epiano variation); occasional lead
- **Vocal**: close-mic SM7B hoặc condenser; **doubled but slightly off** (intentional vibe imperfection)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0–1.5s; tape echo 1/8 dotted on guitar
- **Saturation**: tape saturation entire mix (~−2 to −3dB, soft)
- **Lo-fi tricks**: bitcrusher subtle, hiss tape baseline, occasional vinyl crackle

## 6. Vocal style

- **Tone**: soft, close, almost-spoken; **không belt**; head voice optional cho nữ
- **Phrasing**: relaxed, behind beat, conversational
- **Stack**: lead + 1 doubled (slightly detuned) + occasional 3rd above; KHÔNG 4-part anime stack
- **Ad-lib**: minimal — "ohhh", "mm", "yeah" subtle
- **Vibrato**: minimal (intentional flat)
- **Reverb**: plate 1.2s short-medium; tape echo

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: teen coming-of-age, crushes, awkwardness, summer nostalgia, growing up, mental health
- **Tone**: introspective, gently humorous, self-deprecating
- **Imagery**: bedroom, coffee shop, suburban streets, cigarettes, phone screen, "you used to", college
- **Pronoun**: "I", "you" — diary-style direct
- **Hook short**: 4–8 syllables ("pretty girl", "all the time", "coffee, your mom")
- **Verse câu 6–10 syllables**, chorus câu 5–8
- **Rhyme**: AABB nhẹ, often slant rhyme; not perfect rhymes
- **Concrete teen details**: "your hand-me-down sweater", "Tuesday afternoon", "Chinese takeout"

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng live drum kit — bedroom pop là drum machine define
- Đừng vocal belt cao — close-mic soft là rule
- Đừng master loud — phải DR8+ vintage feel, không streaming-loud
- Đừng câu hook anthem — phải intimate small
- Đừng bridge dramatic modulate — keep simple
- Đừng synth lead aggressive — phải warm pad / soft pluck
- Đừng professionally tight timing — phải có drift human / drag intentional
