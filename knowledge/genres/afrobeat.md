# Afrobeat / Afrobeats — Cookbook đầy đủ

Phân biệt 2 dòng:
- **Afrobeat** (không có "s"): dòng nhạc Fela Kuti sáng lập 1960–70s tại Nigeria, kết hợp
  **West African highlife + funk + jazz + protest lyrics**. Track dài 10–20 phút, polyrhythmic,
  horn-heavy, political.
- **Afrobeats** (có "s"): umbrella term cho **pop music Tây Phi hiện đại** (2010+), bao gồm
  Afro-pop, Afro-fusion, Afro-dancehall, Amapiano. Burna Boy, Wizkid, Davido, Tems, CKay,
  Rema, Ayra Starr. Global hit "Love Nwantiti", "Essence", "Last Last".

File này cover CẢ HAI, chia section rõ.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

### Classic Afrobeat (Fela Kuti)
| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 100–130 BPM (groove-driven, perpetual motion) |
| Time sig | 4/4 với polyrhythm phức tạp (multiple overlapping patterns) |
| Key | Major dominant (Eb, F, G, Bb) |
| Duration | 10–20 phút (extended jam) |
| Master loudness | −14 đến −10 LUFS (dynamic live) |

### Modern Afrobeats (Burna Boy / Wizkid / CKay)
| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 95–115 BPM (Afro-pop); 108–120 BPM (Amapiano) |
| Time sig | 4/4 |
| Key | Minor ~55% (Am, Cm, Em, Fm); major cho feel-good (C, D, F) |
| Vocal range | Nam C3–A4 (Wizkid smooth tenor); nữ A3–E5 (Tems alto-tenor) |
| Master loudness | −7 đến −5 LUFS (streaming-loud) |
| Log drum | Có — Amapiano signature (low-pitched log drum bounce) |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

### Classic Afrobeat
```
Intro / horn riff (8–16 bar)
→ Call-and-response vocal section A (32–64 bar, repeated groove)
→ Instrumental jam (horn solos, guitar solos) (64+ bar)
→ Vocal section B (32–64 bar)
→ Extended outro / jam (32+ bar, fade or stop)
```
**Note**: Fela Kuti track KHÔNG có chorus theo nghĩa pop. Groove lặp, lyrics chant/call-response.

### Modern Afrobeats
```
Intro (4–8, percussion + guitar/synth riff)
→ Verse 1 (8–16, vocal melodic)
→ Pre-hook (4–8)
→ Hook / Chorus (8–16, catchy, singable)
→ Verse 2 (8–16)  →  Pre-hook  →  Hook
→ Bridge (8, vocal ad-lib hoặc beat switch)
→ Final Hook (16, layered)
→ Outro (4–8, percussion fade)
```

## 3. Drum / percussion pattern — polyrhythmic core

### Classic Afrobeat drum pattern (Tony Allen style)
```
Kick:    X . . X . . X . . . X . . X . .   (syncopated, NOT on 1)
Snare:   . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . .   (ghost notes between)
Hi-hat:  X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X   (1/16 constant, accented off-beat)
Shekere: . X . X . X . X . X . X . X . X   (off-beat 1/8)
Conga:   X . X . . X . X X . X . . X . X   (tumbao-influenced)
Bell:    X . . X . . X . . X . . X . . X   (timeline pattern — giữ groove)
```
**Tony Allen** (drummer Fela Kuti) = inventor of Afrobeat drumming. Kick KHÔNG on beat 1 — syncopated.

### Modern Afrobeats (programmed)
```
Kick:    X . . . . . X . X . . . . . X .   (bouncy, syncopated)
Snare:   . . . . X . . . . . . . X . . .   (2+4 or ghost)
Hi-hat:  X . X X . X X . X . X X . X X .   (triplet feel / shuffled 1/16)
Shaker:  X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X   (1/16 constant)
Log drum: . . X . . . . X . . X . . . . X  (Amapiano — low pitched bounce)
```

## 4. Vòng hợp âm điển hình

| Tên vòng | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| Highlife major | I–IV–V–I | G–C–D–G. West African highlife foundation. |
| Afro-pop minor | i–bVI–bIII–bVII | Am–F–C–G. Pop-friendly minor. |
| Afrobeats 2-chord | i–bVII (loop) | Am–G. Hypnotic groove. |
| Amapiano log | i–iv–bVII | Am–Dm–G. Bouncy Amapiano. |
| CKay romantic | I–vi–IV–V | C–Am–F–G. Love song axis. |
| Burna Boy modal | i–bIII–bVII–iv | Am–C–G–Dm. Afro-fusion. |
| Fela horn vamp | I7–IV7 (2-chord) | Eb7–Ab7. Funk-jazz loop. |
| Wizkid dreamy | Imaj7–iii7–vi7–IV | Fmaj7–Am7–Dm7–Bb. Smooth. |

**Guitar**: West African guitar style — arpeggiated, palm-muted, clean tone (Strat/Tele), **highlife picking** pattern (1/16 note arpeggios).

## 5. Phối khí per section

### Classic Afrobeat
| Element | Chi tiết |
|--|--|
| Drums | Kit đầy đủ + conga + shekere + agogo bell + claves. Polyrhythmic. |
| Bass | Electric bass, deep groove, syncopated, follows kick pattern. |
| Guitar | Clean electric (Strat), highlife picking arpeggios, rhythmic. 2 guitars: rhythm + lead. |
| Horns | Trumpet (2–3) + tenor sax (1–2) + baritone sax + trombone. HORN SECTION LÀ SIGNATURE. |
| Keys | Organ (Hammond/Farfisa) comping off-beat. |
| Vocal | Chant, call-response, political speech, Yoruba/Pidgin. |

### Modern Afrobeats
| Section | Lớp âm thanh |
|--|--|
| Intro | Guitar arpeggio (clean, palm-muted) + shaker/percussion + pad mỏng. |
| Verse | Guitar arpeggio, programmed drums (bouncy kick + ghost snare + shuffled hi-hat), bass synth sub, shaker, light pad. Vocal melodic. |
| Pre-hook | Add percussion (conga/djembe), synth pad swell, vocal stack tease. |
| Chorus | Full production: lead guitar riff, full drums + percussion, bass prominent, backing vocal harmony, synth lead (sometimes flute/sax patch). |
| Bridge | Strip-down: percussion + vocal only. Hoặc log drum solo (Amapiano influence). |
| Final Chorus | Everything + vocal ad-libs + horn stab accents + percussion fill. |
| Outro | Percussion fade + guitar arpeggio tail. |

**Amapiano elements** (khi crossover): log drum (pitched low C1–C2), piano stab (Rhodes/Wurlitzer), bassline bouncy.

## 7. Vocal style

### Classic Afrobeat
- **Chant / call-and-response**: Fela sings, chorus repeats
- **Political speech**: spoken word sections mixed with singing
- **Yoruba / Pidgin English**: Nigerian languages dominant

### Modern Afrobeats
- **Melodic singing**: smooth, laid-back, slightly nasal tone (Wizkid signature)
- **Patois influence**: Jamaican dancehall phrasing mixed with Yoruba/Pidgin
- **Vocal ad-libs**: "eh-eh", "shayo", "wahala", scattered throughout
- **Auto-tune**: light-moderate (CKay, Rema); minimal (Burna Boy, Tems)
- **Backing vocal**: 2–3 part harmony, call-response tradition maintained
- **Reverb**: plate 1.0–1.5s, delay 1/4 or dotted 1/8
- **Vocal chop**: short vocal sample loops as textural element

## 8. Lyric idiom

- **Language**: English + Yoruba + Igbo + Pidgin English mix (code-switching tự nhiên)
- **Theme**: love/romance (dominant), party/dance, hustle/ambition, gratitude, identity, social commentary
- **Imagery**: Lagos traffic, beach, night club, hometown village, family
- **Hook**: catchy, repetitive, often bilingual. "Nwantiti", "Essence", "Calm Down" — short phrase loop.
- **Tone**: confident, smooth, celebratory; melancholic khi heartbreak
- **Pidgin examples**: "No be small thing", "Wahala no dey finish", "E dey pain me"
- **Code-switch**: chorus English-dominant cho global reach, verse mix Yoruba/Pidgin

## 10. DON'T

- KHÔNG bỏ percussion layer — Afrobeat/Afrobeats = polyrhythm, cần ít nhất 3 percussion elements.
- KHÔNG để guitar clean quá "rock" — cần palm-mute + arpeggio highlife style.
- KHÔNG dùng straight 4-on-floor kick — Afrobeats kick syncopated, bouncy.
- KHÔNG skip code-switching — bilingual lyrics là signature Afrobeats.
- KHÔNG nhầm Afrobeat (Fela) với Afrobeats (modern pop) — chúng related nhưng rất khác.
