# Classical / Cinematic Orchestration

Dòng nhạc instrumental classical pop / cinematic score. Đại diện modern: Ludovico Einaudi
(Italian piano minimalist), Hans Zimmer (epic film score Inception/Interstellar/Dunkirk),
Yiruma (Korean piano romantic), Max Richter (post-classical), John Williams (Hollywood
classic Star Wars/Harry Potter), Joe Hisaishi (anime film score Ghibli), Ramin Djawadi
(Game of Thrones). Đặc trưng: orchestra full balance hoặc piano minimalist, tempo 60-100 BPM,
chord progression simple but powerful, dynamic arc dramatic.

## Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | Adagio 60-72 BPM, Andante 72-100 BPM, Moderato 100-120 BPM, Allegro 120+ BPM |
| Key | Major key uplifting (C, G, D, F); minor key dramatic (Cm, Gm, Dm, Am, Em); modulate +1/+2 dramatic |
| Time sig | 4/4 (most common); 3/4 (waltz Einaudi); 6/8 (lyrical); 5/4 (cinematic exotic Zimmer) |
| Form | Free-form theme + variation; A-B-A (ternary); through-composed (no repeat) |
| Duration | 2:30-6:00 (single movement); 5-15 min (cinematic suite) |

## Cấu trúc song-form

### Ternary form (most common modern)

```
A (1-2 minute) — main theme stated, sparse texture
B (1-2 minute) — contrasting middle, different key/tempo, dynamic shift
A' (1-2 minute) — return to A, fuller orchestration, climax
Coda (30-60 sec) — fade or final cadence
```

### Theme + variation

```
Theme (sparse, solo piano hoặc 1 instrument) — 30-60 sec
Variation 1 (add string section) — 30-60 sec
Variation 2 (add brass + woodwind) — 30-60 sec
Variation 3 (full orchestra climax) — 30-60 sec
Variation 4 (strip back, intimate close) — 30-60 sec
```

## Vòng hợp âm

### Cinematic minor (epic feel)

```
i – bVII – bVI – bVII (Am – G – F – G) — Hans Zimmer signature drone
i – bVI – bIII – bVII (Am – F – C – G) — anthemic
i – v – bVI – iv (Am – Em – F – Dm) — emotional descent
```

### Classical major (uplifting)

```
I – V – vi – IV (axis "Pachelbel" related)
I – vi – ii – V (jazz-influenced)
I – iii – IV – I (Bach-influenced)
```

### Classical Baroque cycle (Pachelbel signature)

```
D – A – Bm – F#m – G – D – G – A (Pachelbel Canon in D)
```

### Modal mixture (chromatic mediant cinematic)

```
C – Eb – Ab – G (parallel minor borrow, cinematic Hans Zimmer)
C – Am – F – G – Em7 – Am – Dm – G (Pachelbel + chromatic)
```

## Phối khí — Orchestra balance

### String section (foundation)

| Section | Range | Role |
|--|--|--|
| Violin 1 | G3-E7 | Lead melody, top voice |
| Violin 2 | G3-A6 | Counter-melody hoặc harmony 3rd below Violin 1 |
| Viola | C3-E6 | Inner voice, alto bridge between Violin 2 and Cello |
| Cello | C2-A5 | Bass line + tenor counter |
| Double Bass | E1-G3 | Octave below Cello, fundamental bass |

**Voicing rule**: Vln1 + Vln2 octave doubling lead melody = full sound. Vla bridges middle.
Cello + Bass octave for low foundation.

### Brass section

| Instrument | Range | Role |
|--|--|--|
| Trumpet 1 | E4-D6 | Lead brass voice, fanfare |
| Trumpet 2 | E4-D6 | Harmony 3rd below |
| French Horn | F2-F5 | Pastoral lead OR brass pad |
| Trombone | E2-F5 | Tenor brass, bass melody |
| Tuba | F1-F4 | Bass foundation brass |

### Woodwind section

| Instrument | Range | Role |
|--|--|--|
| Flute | C4-D7 | Lyrical lead, ornaments |
| Oboe | B♭3-A6 | Melancholic solo voice |
| Clarinet | E3-C7 | Versatile lead/inner voice |
| Bassoon | B♭1-E5 | Bass woodwind, comedic/lyric |

### Percussion + Harp

- Timpani (drum tuned to chord roots, 4 tom F2-F3 typical) — cinematic dramatic rolls
- Harp — arpeggio chord voicing 1/16 ascending (signature)
- Cymbal swell — build climax
- Tom rolls — military / dramatic

### Piano (solo classical or modern)

- Often solo / featured (Einaudi, Yiruma)
- Range A0-C8 full keyboard
- Voicing: bass octave LH (left hand) + chord melody RH (right hand)

## Lyric / Vocal (when applicable)

Most cinematic / classical instrumental KHÔNG có lyric. When vocal added:

- **Wordless vocal** (oh, ah, mmm) — Lisa Gerrard "Now We Are Free" (Gladiator)
- **Latin / Italian** — sacred choir feel ("Lacrimosa", "Sanctus")
- **Solo soprano** ethereal — Enya "Only Time"
- **Boys choir** — Hans Zimmer "Time"
- **Choir SATB** — Lord of the Rings "Soundtrack"
- KHÔNG English specific lyric (movie score = universal)

## Dynamic arc (signature classical)

```
ppp (very soft, almost silent) → pp → p → mp → mf → f → ff → fff (very loud climax)

Build:
- Section 1 (0-30s): ppp solo piano
- Section 2 (30-60s): p add cello + violin 1
- Section 3 (60-90s): mp add full strings + flute
- Section 4 (90-120s): mf add brass + woodwind
- Section 5 (120-150s): f add timpani + cymbal swell
- Section 6 (150-180s): ff full orchestra + harp arpeggio CLIMAX
- Section 7 (180-210s): mp strip back, return solo piano
- Section 8 (210-240s): pp final, fade
```

## DO / DON'T

**DO**:
- Dynamic arc dramatic ppp → ff → pp (signature classical)
- String voicing octave doubling Vln1+Vln2 lead
- Bass foundation Cello + Double Bass octave low
- Chord progression simple but powerful (I-V-vi-IV axis hoặc minor i-bVII-bVI-bVII)
- Modulate +1 hoặc +2 dramatic shift cho climax
- Tempo rubato (slight tempo flex within phrase)
- Solo piano option (Einaudi, Yiruma minimalist)
- Wordless vocal hoặc Latin choir (when applicable)
- Timpani rolls for dramatic build
- Harp arpeggio 1/16 signature ornament

**DON'T**:
- KHÔNG 4-on-floor kick (classical NOT EDM)
- KHÔNG synthesizer modern (use real orchestra or sampled orchestra: VSL/Spitfire/Cinematic Studio)
- KHÔNG autotune
- KHÔNG drum kit pop standard (chỉ timpani + cymbal + tom rolls cinematic)
- KHÔNG vocal pop English lyric specific
- KHÔNG hook lặp K-pop style — classical phát triển theme + variation
- KHÔNG dembow / shuffle / jazz swing rhythm
- KHÔNG static dynamics (classical phải arc)

## Persona linkage

- **Theorist**: tempo Adagio 60-72 / Andante 72-100 / Moderato 100-120 BPM. Key major C/G/D/F
  uplifting hoặc minor Cm/Gm/Dm/Am/Em dramatic. Chord I-V-vi-IV axis hoặc i-bVII-bVI-bVII
  cinematic minor; Pachelbel cycle; modal mixture cinematic. Modulate +1/+2 dramatic.
- **Composer**: form ternary A-B-A hoặc theme+variation; KHÔNG strict 32-bar verse-chorus.
  Phrase length flexible, theme 4-8 bar repeated with variation. Tempo rubato
  (slight flex). Dynamic arc ppp → ff → pp BẮT BUỘC.
- **Lyricist**: nếu có vocal — wordless (oh/ah/mmm), Latin choir, hoặc solo soprano. KHÔNG
  English specific lyric. Most pieces instrumental only.
- **Arranger**: orchestra full balance: Vln1+Vln2 octave doubling lead, Vla bridge alto,
  Cello+DBass octave bass; brass fanfare voicing; harp arpeggio 1/16; timpani rolls;
  woodwind flute/oboe solo. Dynamic arc ppp → ff → pp.
- **Producer**: LUFS −16 to −14 (classical preserves wide dynamics, NO over-limiting);
  hall reverb 2.5-3.5s (concert hall) or chamber 1.8s; orchestra panning realistic
  (Vln1 left, Vln2 left-center, Vla center, Cello center-right, Bass right; brass back, winds
  sides, harp far right); reference Einaudi / Hans Zimmer / Yiruma / John Williams /
  Joe Hisaishi.
