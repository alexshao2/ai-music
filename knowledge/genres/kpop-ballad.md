# K-pop Ballad — Cookbook đầy đủ

K-pop ballad (~2010+) là dòng emotional vocal-driven, intimate hơn idol-pop, tập trung vào
vocal kỹ thuật (Taeyeon belt E5+, IU head voice F#5+) + production piano-led với strings real
+ R&B influence rõ. Khác V-pop ballad ở chỗ vocal bel canto kỹ thuật cao hơn, chord motion phức
tạp hơn, master loudness hot hơn.

## 1. Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 60–90 BPM. Slow 60–75 (IU "Through the Night"); mid 75–90 (Taeyeon "Fine") |
| Time sig | 4/4 (~85%); 6/8 cho rocking ballad (Taeyeon "Rain") |
| Key | Major dominant ~60% (D, F, G, A); minor cho heartbreak (Em, Am, Bm) |
| Vocal range | Nữ E4–G5 (IU E4–F#5 head voice; Taeyeon E4–G5 belt); nam C3–A4 falsetto (Crush, Park Hyo Shin) |
| Master loudness | −7 đến −9 LUFS (loud hơn V-pop ballad −9 to −11) |
| Texture | Felt grand piano + lush strings (real) + brushed drums + bass + R&B Rhodes layer |

## 2. Cấu trúc bài (form)

```
Intro (4–8 bar, piano arpeggio hoặc strings swell)
→ Verse 1 (16, piano + close-mic vocal)  →  Pre-chorus (8, build with strings)
→ Chorus (16, full kit + belt)
→ Verse 2 (16, add R&B Rhodes layer)  →  Pre  →  Chorus
→ Bridge (8–16, strip-down piano + vocal HOẶC modulate +1 mid-bridge)
→ Final Chorus (16, modulate +1 hoặc layer max)  →  Outro (4–8 fade hoặc final cadence)
```

**K-pop ballad signature**: thường modulate +1 (nửa cung) trước final chorus — emotional lift
mạnh nhất. IU thường KHÔNG modulate (giống Adele); Taeyeon, Crush thường modulate.

## 3. Vòng hợp âm hay

K-pop ballad mượn từ R&B + jazz hơn pop ballad Tây:

| Tên | Pattern | Ví dụ |
|--|--|--|
| K-ballad axis | I V vi IV (axis pop ballad) | C G Am F |
| K-R&B ballad | Imaj7 vi9 IVmaj7 V7 | Cmaj7 Am9 Fmaj7 G7 |
| Modal interchange | I bVI IV V | C Ab F G (borrowed bVI) |
| Secondary dominant | I V/vi vi IV | C E7 Am F |
| Modulation final | I → +1 via dominant lift | C → C#m via G7→C# pivot |
| Picardy | i to I final cadence | Am → Amaj surprise resolution |

**Trick**: chord change every 2 bar verse, every 1 bar chorus dồn; **suspended chord** (Csus2,
Fadd9) intro để mở mood gentle.

## 4. Phối khí per section

- **Intro**: piano grand (Steinway/Bösendorfer close-mic) + strings swell light; vocal "ohhh" optional
- **Verse 1**: piano + close-mic vocal + bass enter bar 9; KHÔNG drums yet
- **Pre-chorus**: thêm strings legato + light brushed drums (kick 1+3, brushed snare 2+4 with reverb 0.5s)
- **Chorus 1**: piano big + strings full + bass + drums (kick 1+3, snare 2+4 reverb 0.6s); vocal stack 2-part
- **Verse 2**: piano + bass + drums tiếp; thêm Rhodes EP layer cho R&B color
- **Bridge**: strip-down piano + vocal only (drama through silence) HOẶC big drum drop with belt
- **Final Chorus**: modulate +1 OR add gospel choir + tom rolls + counter-melody piano right hand
- **Outro**: piano tag với vocal "ohhh" hoặc final whisper "I love you"

## 6. Vocal style

- **Tone**: pure crystal-clear (IU); soulful belt (Taeyeon); raspy R&B (Crush); rich baritone (Park Hyo Shin)
- **Phrasing**: precise on-beat (less back-phrasing than English ballad)
- **Melisma**: significant — K-pop singers love long melismas (1 syllable across 4-8 notes)
- **Stack**: lead solo verse → +1 doubled pre → +3rd above chorus → +5th + ad-lib chorus 2/final
- **Ad-libs**: gospel-style runs in final chorus; "yeah", "oh-oh-oh"
- **Reverb**: plate 1.2–1.5s decay, predelay 25–35ms; subtle slap delay 1/4 dotted on tail

## 7. Lyric idiom

- **Theme**: love (long-distance, departed, unspoken), heartbreak, longing, friendship, memory
- **Pronoun**: 너 (neo, "you" informal — most common); 나 (na, "I"); 우리 (uri, "we"); 그대 (geudae, "you" formal/poetic)
- **Imagery Korean**: 비 (bi, rain), 별 (byeol, star), 밤 (bam, night), 달 (dal, moon), 봄 (bom, spring),
  꽃 (kkot, flower), 편지 (pyeonji, letter), 기억 (gieok, memory), 거리 (geori, street)
- **English code-mix subtle**: 1-2 từ tiếng Anh trong chorus ("baby", "love", "yeah") — ít hơn idol-pop
- **Hook line** 4–8 syllables Korean: "그날의 우리" (geunalui uri, "us on that day"), "11:11" tag
- **Verse câu 8–12 syllables**, chorus câu 6–10
- **Rhyme**: Korean tail rhyme với 했어/없어/봤어 (haesseo/eopseo/bwasseo) — ㅓ vowel ending common

## 9. DON'T (đừng)

- Đừng vocal flat — K-pop ballad expect bel canto melisma, head voice mở
- Đừng skip strings — real string section essential (sample chấp nhận nếu sample chuẩn VSL/Spitfire)
- Đừng modulate +2 (full step) — quá lộ; +1 semitone mới đúng K-pop ballad signature
- Đừng English code-mix nặng — K-pop ballad < idol-pop về tỷ lệ English (giữ ≤15%)
- Đừng cliché "마음이 아파" (maeumi apa, "my heart hurts") — direct Korean cliché
- Đừng bridge 4 bar — phải 8–16 bar emotional pivot
