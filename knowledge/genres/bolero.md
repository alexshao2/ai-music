# Bolero Việt

Bolero là dòng nhạc trữ tình nguồn gốc Cuba/Latin du nhập VN từ thập niên 1950, phát triển
mạnh ở miền Nam VN qua Phạm Duy, Trúc Phương, Hoàng Thi Thơ, Lam Phương. Ngày nay có
revival mạnh qua Lệ Quyên, Đan Nguyên, Quang Lê, Phương Mỹ Chi, Hương Lan. Đặc trưng: tempo
slow 60-80 BPM, organ + guitar lead + drum brushed, vibrato sâu, lyric kể chuyện tha hương /
tình cũ / mẹ / quê hương.

## Tham số âm thanh

| Tham số | Giá trị |
|--|--|
| Tempo | 60–80 BPM (sweet-spot 70 BPM) |
| Key | Major key dominant (C, G, F, D, A) — bolero gốc Cuba major; minor cũng dùng (Am, Em) |
| Time sig | 4/4 chủ yếu (slow ballad); 3/4 cũng dùng (waltz feel rare) |
| Form | Intro – Verse 1 – Verse 2 – Chorus – Verse 3 – Chorus – Bridge – Final Chorus – Outro |
| Duration | 4:00–5:30 (bolero thường dài, kể chuyện thong thả) |

## Cấu trúc song-form (bolero classic)

```
Intro (8 bar) — organ + guitar arpeggio chord progression
Verse 1 (16 bar) — vocal kể chuyện nhân vật
Verse 2 (16 bar) — vocal phát triển cảm xúc
Chorus (16 bar) — vocal tâm sự mạnh hơn, nâng register
Verse 3 (16 bar) — vocal hồi tưởng quá khứ
Chorus (16 bar) — repeat with vocal stack 2-part light
Bridge (8-16 bar) — instrumental guitar lead solo (signature) hoặc chord shift
Final Chorus (16 bar) — vocal full belt, vibrato max
Outro (8 bar) — fade với guitar arpeggio + organ sustain
```

## Vòng hợp âm

### Bolero major (most common)

```
I – vi – ii – V (C – Am – Dm – G7) — circle of fifths classic
I – iii – IV – V (C – Em – F – G)
I – V/vi – vi – IV (C – E7 – Am – F) — secondary dominant for emotional pivot
```

### Bolero minor (sad theme)

```
i – iv – v – i (Am – Dm – Em – Am) — pure minor
i – iv – V7 – i (Am – Dm – E7 – Am) — harmonic minor with V7 leading
i – bVI – iv – V7 (Am – F – Dm – E7) — dramatic
```

### Bolero turnaround / cadence

```
ii – V7 – I (Dm – G7 – C) — strong jazz cadence at end of phrase
IV – V – I (F – G – C) — plagal-style strong
```

## Drum / rhythm pattern

**Brushed drums** (signature bolero sound — not stick drums):
```
Kick:   X . . . . . X . X . . . . . . . (off-beat 1, 7)
Snare:  . . X . . . . . . . X . . . . . (brushed sweep beat 3, 11)
HiHat:  X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X (1/16 sustained brush)
```

Hoặc **slow boléro Latin pattern**:
```
Kick:   X . . . X . . . X . . . X . . .
Snare (light):  . . X . . . . X . . X . . . . X
Maracas: X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X X
Conga (subtle): . X . X . . X . X . . X . X . .
```

## Phối khí (instrumentation)

### Intro

- Hammond organ (Leslie speaker rotating) sustained chord
- Classical/Spanish guitar arpeggio fingerpicking
- Brushed drums very light kick + snare brushes
- Sometimes solo flute hoặc violin lead-in

### Verse

- Hammond organ sustained pad
- Classical guitar arpeggio 1/8 fingerpick
- Bass guitar walking subtle (or contrabass acoustic)
- Brushed drums light
- Vocal lead, vibrato medium

### Chorus

- Add string section (2nd violin + viola + cello)
- Add accordion (signature Latin element) playing chord melody
- Drums slightly louder brushed
- Vocal stack 2-part (lead + 3rd above)
- Plate verb 1.6s

### Bridge / Instrumental break

- **GUITAR LEAD SOLO** (Spanish/classical style fingerstyle 16 bar) — SIGNATURE bolero element
- Or saxophone solo (jazz-influenced bolero)
- Or violin solo (sentimental)
- Drums lock pattern, bass walking
- Vocal silent, instrumental focus

### Final Chorus

- All elements + tom rolls
- Vocal full belt, vibrato max
- Plate verb 1.8s
- Maybe modulate +1 semitone (rare in bolero, but Lệ Quyên uses it)

### Outro

- Fade with guitar arpeggio + organ sustain + bass long note
- Drum tail brushed
- Vocal "ah" sustained final note

## Vocal style

- **Vibrato sâu** (slow 4-6 Hz, wide ±50 cent) — signature bolero
- Belting NHẸ (not full pop belt) — emotional restraint, classy
- Long sustains 4-8 beat, fade với vibrato
- **Slide / portamento** entry to note (signature)
- Breath audible — không pitch correct heavy
- Vocal range: nữ A3-D5 (alto/mezzo); nam C3-F4 (baritone/tenor)
- KHÔNG autotune — bolero giữ tính tự nhiên human

## Lyric idiom

- **Storytelling 1st-person** — "tôi", "anh", "em", kể chuyện tha hương
- Theme phổ biến: tình cũ / quê hương / mẹ / chiến tranh / ly biệt / mưa
- Vần lưng (internal rhyme) phổ biến
- Vần chân (end-of-line rhyme) -ày / -ơ / -ương / -ai
- Imagery: bến đò, dòng sông, phố cũ, mưa rơi, áo lụa, ngọn đèn, gánh hàng rong
- Pronoun consistent: "em" (sweetheart) / "anh" (lover) / "mẹ" (mother) / "quê" (home)
- KHÔNG dùng English code-mix (bolero pure VN tradition)

## DO / DON'T

**DO**:
- Tempo slow 60-80 BPM strict
- Vocal vibrato sâu 4-6 Hz (signature)
- Guitar lead solo 16-bar instrumental break (signature)
- Brushed drums (NOT stick drums)
- Hammond organ pad sustained
- Storytelling lyric VN tha hương / tình cũ
- Pure VN lyric (NO English code-mix)

**DON'T**:
- KHÔNG tempo > 90 BPM (đó là V-pop ballad, không phải bolero)
- KHÔNG dùng autotune (bolero giữ tự nhiên)
- KHÔNG vocal stack > 2-part (bolero giữ intimate)
- KHÔNG English code-mix
- KHÔNG modern hi-hat 1/16 (đó là R&B / pop, không phải bolero)
- KHÔNG drum stick aggressive — brushed only
- KHÔNG synth lead modern — classical instrument only

## Persona linkage

- **Theorist**: tempo 60-80 BPM, key C/G/F/D/A major hoặc Am/Em/Bm minor, ii-V-I cadence,
  secondary dominant emotional pivot. Modulate +1 hiếm khi (modern bolero Lệ Quyên).
- **Composer**: form Intro/V1/V2/Chorus/V3/Chorus/Bridge instrumental 16 bar/Final/Outro.
  Vocal range alto C4-D5 (nữ), baritone C3-F4 (nam). Phrase 8-12 syllable verse, 6-10 syllable chorus.
- **Lyricist**: storytelling 1st-person, theme tha hương / tình cũ / mẹ / quê. Pronoun nhất quán
  "em"/"anh"/"tôi". Vần chân -ày / -ơ / -ương / -ai. KHÔNG English code-mix.
- **Arranger**: Hammond organ + classical guitar fingerpick + brushed drums + accordion +
  string section. Bridge: GUITAR LEAD SOLO 16 bar (signature). Tom rolls subtle final.
- **Producer**: LUFS −10 to −12 (warm vintage); plate verb 1.6-1.8s; analog tape saturation;
  vocal vibrato preserved (NO autotune); reference Lệ Quyên / Đan Nguyên / Quang Lê /
  Khánh Ly classic.
